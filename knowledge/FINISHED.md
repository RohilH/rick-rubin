# Is this track finished?

One checklist merged from the creators' feedback and fix-a-track videos: Ryos's feedback stream (13 tracks; the
counts below are how many of the 12 criticised tracks failed that point) and his subscriber fix, Alex Rome's
"pro sound", weak-bass and two mixing videos, and Moonboy's genre sheets. Go through it in this order: a mix
cannot rescue a wrong sound, and a master cannot rescue a mix. Each line: what to check; **M** how the copilot
can measure it (or "by ear only"); **Fix** the cards. Paths are relative to `knowledge/`. The themes behind it
are in [PRINCIPLES.md](PRINCIPLES.md). A track that passes every line can still sound flat: finish with a bounce
for Rohil and his verdict.

## 0. Before pressing play
1. Look at the waveform: tiny means unmastered, a brick means crushed (3 of 12, plus 2). **M** integrated loudness and crest against the references. **Fix** [clipper, kick at zero](techniques/mastering/ryos-produce-into-a-clipper-kick-at-zero.md).

## 1. Idea and notes
2. Do the main one or two sounds state the mood with nothing else playing? **M** by ear only (bounce the two alone). **Fix** [two sounds](techniques/mindset/ryos-b-two-sounds-must-carry-the-feeling.md).
3. Is every part in one key, with the bass on the chords' roots? **M** `notes` on lead, chord and bass stems: one scale, same roots. **Fix** [open voicings, one key](techniques/harmony/ryos-open-voicings-simple-triads-in-key.md), [one progression feeds every part](techniques/harmony/moonboy-b-one-progression-feeds-every-part.md).
4. Is everything on a 4/4 grid? **M** onsets against the 16th grid (`hits`). **Fix** [Ryos's checklist](techniques/workflow/ryos-finished-track-checklist.md).
5. Do break and drop share a progression, or has the drop's harmony been heard under the hook? **M** chord and bass roots of both sections. **Fix** [one progression for break and drop](techniques/harmony/ryos-b-one-progression-for-break-and-drop.md).
6. Does the melody repeat a rhythm the ear can predict, and can it be hummed after two listens? (6 of 12 needed simpler or corrected notes.) **M** onset positions per bar (`score`); the hum test is by ear. **Fix** [rhythm symmetry](techniques/melody/ryos-melody-rhythm-symmetry.md), [melody bank and walk-away test](techniques/workflow/ryos-c-melody-bank-and-walk-away-test.md).
7. Is only one part busy? **M** onsets per bar per element: one clearly ahead. **Fix** [one fast part](techniques/arrangement/ryos-c-one-fast-part-everything-else-slower.md).
8. Are the chords spread (third away from the bass, a chosen top note), with no chord too many? **M** `score` for the voicing; chord energy in the octave above the bass fundamental. **Fix** [ten progressions and three voicing moves](techniques/harmony/alex-rome-ten-progressions-overview.md), [open voicings](techniques/harmony/ryos-open-voicings-simple-triads-in-key.md).
9. Does the bass support the melody: the rhythm of the part above it, small gaps between notes, passing notes where the melody rests? **M** bass onsets against lead onsets. **Fix** [bassline from the chords](techniques/bass/alex-rome-b-bassline-from-the-chords-bottom-notes.md), [passing notes](techniques/bass/alex-rome-c-bass-passing-notes-in-the-melody-gaps.md).
10. Which melody role drives each section, and is one missing (often the accent)? **M** by reading `score`: one dense line, the rest sparser. **Fix** [five melody roles](techniques/melody/alex-rome-c-five-melody-roles-overview.md).

## 2. Sounds
11. Does every sound belong to this genre and mood? (6 of 12.) **M** by ear; per-band balance against the genre's references is a hint. **Fix** [pro-sound audit](techniques/workflow/alex-rome-c-pro-sound-audit-lead-chords-bass-drums.md).
12. Kick: right for the genre, thick in solo, with a body that lasts? **M** `audition`: length and low-band energy late in the sample. **Fix** [kick for the genre](techniques/drums/ryos-kick-that-fits-the-genre.md), [kick with a full tail](techniques/drums/alex-rome-c-kick-with-a-full-tail-half-a-beat-long.md).
13. Is the lead bright enough? **M** lead stem's energy above 4 kHz and harmonic count, against the references. **Fix** [brighten with harmonics](techniques/sound-design/ryos-brighten-a-dull-lead-with-harmonics.md).
14. Does the lead have body near 300 Hz and a centred layer under its width? **M** lead stem at 200 to 500 Hz; side % per layer; correlation at or above zero. **Fix** [centre anchor, then widen](techniques/sound-design/ryos-lead-stack-centre-anchor-then-widen.md).
15. Does each layer do a job the others do not? **M** per-layer band energy: each peaks elsewhere; muting one moves a band. **Fix** [layer by contrast](techniques/sound-design/ryos-layer-by-contrast.md), [lead stack roles](techniques/sound-design/ryos-pg-lead-stack-six-roles.md).
16. Do held sounds move (a decay, a filter, a gate)? **M** stem level and brightness over time: not flat. **Fix** [chords that decay](techniques/sound-design/alex-rome-c-chords-that-decay-like-a-piano.md), [wave, filter, movement](techniques/sound-design/moonboy-wavetable-filter-movement.md).
17. Is the energy continuous between the lead's notes? **M** lead level between notes does not fall to silence. **Fix** [pluck lead with delay](techniques/sound-design/alex-rome-c-pluck-lead-with-delay-to-fill-the-gaps.md), [OTT after the reverb](techniques/mixing/alex-rome-c-ott-before-or-after-reverb.md).
18. Are the drop's sounds harder than the break's versions of them? **M** drop against break: lower crest, more high-mid energy. **Fix** [harden break sounds](techniques/arrangement/ryos-c-harden-break-sounds-for-the-drop.md).

## 3. Arrangement and energy
19. Does it get to the point: vocal and hook early, an intro that previews the parts filtered? (3 of 12.) **M** time to first vocal and first drop against the references; intro energy under 80 Hz and above 8 kHz well below the drop's. **Fix** [intro that previews](techniques/arrangement/ryos-intro-previews-filtered-gets-to-the-point.md).
20. Does something nameable change at every eight-bar boundary? (4 of 12.) **M** elements that start or stop at each boundary; near-identical two-bar blocks and texture changes per minute against the references. **Fix** [every eight bars](techniques/arrangement/ryos-something-significant-every-eight-bars.md), [two-loop rule](techniques/arrangement/alex-rome-b-two-loop-rule.md).
21. Does the drop's second half have more than its first, and is the drop more than one loop? **M** top-end energy of each half; near-identical blocks inside the drop. **Fix** [second-pass variations](techniques/arrangement/ryos-b-second-pass-variations-in-a-drop.md), [A and B drop](techniques/arrangement/moonboy-a-b-drop-sections.md), [call and response](techniques/arrangement/ryos-b-call-and-response-drop-two-groups.md).
22. Does the break start sparse and grow, with an atmosphere bed under it? **M** loudness and high band step up from its first half to its second; low band near empty at the start. **Fix** [strip the break](techniques/arrangement/ryos-b-strip-the-break-then-add-in-stages.md), [entries by frequency](techniques/arrangement/ryos-c-break-entries-ordered-by-frequency.md).
23. Are there enough layers: something between bass and lead, sparkle on top? Does the section shrink when the vocal stops? (5 of 12.) **M** no dip from 300 Hz to 2 kHz; energy above 8 kHz in the break; loudness with the vocal stem muted. **Fix** [fill the mid range](techniques/arrangement/ryos-fill-the-mid-range-and-atmosphere-bed.md), [hidden percussion loop](techniques/sound-design/alex-rome-c-hidden-percussion-loop-under-the-music.md), [hidden drone](techniques/sound-design/alex-rome-c-hidden-drone-from-a-bounced-reverb.md).
24. Does the build filter the old section away and imply the drop? **M** low band falls and brightness rises bar by bar; the step at the drop against the references. **Fix** [build by filtering](techniques/arrangement/ryos-pg-build-by-filtering-everything-away.md), [build previews the drop](techniques/arrangement/ryos-c-build-previews-the-drop-bass-included.md), [crossover](techniques/arrangement/alex-rome-b-crossover-section-with-partial-parts.md).
25. Has every join been played across and judged a satisfying change? (6 of 12 lacked transition detail.) **M** a band or element that appears or disappears across the join; the rest by ear. **Fix** [two checks](techniques/workflow/alex-rome-b-two-checks-before-moving-on.md), [transition details](techniques/arrangement/ryos-transition-details-reverses-cuts-risers.md), [opposite automation](techniques/arrangement/alex-rome-b-opposite-automation-handoff.md).
26. Do the drums develop: staged entries, hats and shakers joining, a fill at phrase ends? **M** top-end activity per eight bars. **Fix** [drums in stages](techniques/drums/alex-rome-b-drums-added-in-stages.md), [shaker and top loop at bar five](techniques/drums/moonboy-b-shakers-and-top-loop-enter-halfway.md).
27. Picture a DJ playing it: where do they mix in and out, and is the break or the drop an event when it comes? **M** by ear only; section lengths against the references. **Fix** [intro that previews](techniques/arrangement/ryos-intro-previews-filtered-gets-to-the-point.md).

## 4. Low end
28. Does the bass sit in the right octave, with weight near 60 Hz and not only a ring at 80 to 120? (Kick and bass: 8 of 12.) **M** strongest peak per bass note between 40 and 80 Hz. **Fix** [bass octave](techniques/bass/alex-rome-c-bass-octave-by-the-analyzer-40-80hz.md), [bass in layers](techniques/bass/ryos-bass-in-layers-sub-harmonics-mid.md).
29. Does the sub have harmonics above it, so it is heard on small speakers? **M** energy share above the sub band in the bass stems; harmonics with no gap. **Fix** [sine sub with distortion](techniques/sound-design/alex-rome-sine-sub-with-distortion.md), [four-layer bass](techniques/bass/ryos-pg-four-layer-bass-stack.md).
30. Does one layer own the sub, with everything else cut out of it and the low end mono? **M** energy under 100 Hz per stem; side % under 150 Hz near zero. **Fix** [low-cut everything](techniques/mixing/alex-rome-low-cut-everything-but-kick-and-bass.md), [wide bass layer](techniques/sound-design/alex-rome-c-wide-bass-layer-with-chorus.md).
31. Do you feel the kick, then the bass? Does the bass dip out on the kick without the whole mix pumping? **M** bass level per 16th lowest on kick steps; pump figure inside the references' range; peak of kick plus bass within about 1 dB of the kick alone. **Fix** [Kickstart sized to the kick](techniques/mixing/alex-rome-c-kickstart-on-the-instrument-group-keyed-by-the-kick.md), [sub duck traced from the kick](techniques/mixing/ryos-c-sub-duck-curve-traced-from-the-kick.md).
32. Is any downbeat carrying a kick, a sub boom and a bass at once? **M** energy under 100 Hz and crest on those beats. **Fix** [pick two](techniques/mixing/ryos-b-kick-sub-boom-bass-pick-two.md).
33. Can the sub be followed from start to end of the section? **M** sub-band level over time: no drop-outs. **Fix** [four-pass mix](techniques/mixing/alex-rome-c-four-pass-mix-eq-ott-sidechain-reference.md).
34. Do pads and drones pump only where there is a kick? **M** their stem level in the break (steady) and the drop (dips each beat). **Fix** [pump depth by section](techniques/mixing/ryos-pg-pump-depth-by-section.md).

## 5. Mix
35. Is the star in front and the bass under it? (Lead not in front: 10 of 12.) **M** loudness order of the stems; the same order in the references. **Fix** fader first, then [one melody in front](techniques/mixing/alex-rome-c-one-melody-in-front-before-adding-bass.md), [clear 200 to 500 Hz](techniques/mixing/ryos-clear-200-500hz-pocket-for-the-lead.md).
36. Do bass and chords leave 200 to 500 Hz to the lead? (5 of 12.) **M** energy per stem in that band; side % of the bass there. **Fix** [clear 200 to 500 Hz](techniques/mixing/ryos-clear-200-500hz-pocket-for-the-lead.md).
37. Are supporting sounds thinned to their one band, each low cut set while the bass plays? **M** energy under each cut; low-mid share of the mix against the references. **Fix** [low cut against the bass](techniques/mixing/alex-rome-c-find-the-low-cut-against-the-bass.md), [low mids set distance](techniques/mixing/ryos-c-low-mids-set-distance-cut-what-is-masked.md).
38. Are reverbs filtered, and is the lead's reverb behind it, not on it? **M** side energy in the low and low-mid bands; lead onsets still sharp. **Fix** [EQ the reverb](techniques/mixing/alex-rome-eq-the-reverb.md), [lead reverb on a send](techniques/mixing/ryos-lead-reverb-send-predelay-duck-glue.md).
39. Are pianos, lead groups and break vocals even enough to sit over the bass? (5 of 12.) **M** crest per stem. **Fix** [slam melodic groups](techniques/mixing/ryos-slam-melodic-groups-with-compression.md), [OTT by role](techniques/mixing/alex-rome-c-ott-amount-by-role.md).
40. Drum balance: kick and clap on top, ghost hits about 6 dB under, cymbals and rides far under; peaks clipped, not squashed? **M** level per step; kick still the loudest step; drum group crest. **Fix** [drum level guide](techniques/mixing/moonboy-auto-pan-rides-and-drum-level-guide.md), [drum group soft clip](techniques/mixing/moonboy-drum-group-soft-clip.md).
41. Is the top end inside its budget, with no single harsh frequency? **M** share above 8 kHz against the references; one peak standing out of a stem's spectrum. **Fix** [noise budget](techniques/mixing/ryos-pg-top-end-noise-budget.md), [dynamic EQ](techniques/mixing/ryos-b-dynamic-eq-for-one-harsh-frequency.md).
42. Is the width under control? **M** side % per band (rising with frequency), correlation at or above zero on every wide stem, mono loss, against the references. **Fix** [keep wide layers in phase](techniques/mixing/ryos-c-keep-wide-layers-in-phase.md), [balance in mono](techniques/mixing/alex-rome-balance-in-mono.md).
43. Break vocal: bright, wide, de-essed, and are the words still there after the effects? **M** vocal stem's top band and side %; `transcribe` finds the words. **Fix** [brighten with harmonics](techniques/sound-design/ryos-brighten-a-dull-lead-with-harmonics.md), [vocoder chain](techniques/sound-design/moonboy-vocoder-vocal-chain.md).
44. Was it mixed in its own session, with same-job layers consolidated? **M** not measurable. **Fix** [mix on a different day](techniques/mixing/alex-rome-mix-on-a-different-day.md), [consolidate layers](techniques/mixing/alex-rome-consolidate-layers-before-mixing.md).

## 6. Loudness
45. Is there a clipper or limiter on the master with the kick at 0 dB, and is the result neither quiet nor crushed? **M** integrated loudness, crest and clipped samples against the references. **Fix** [clipper, kick at zero](techniques/mastering/ryos-produce-into-a-clipper-kick-at-zero.md).
46. Against a same-genre reference at matched loudness: as bright, bass as level, kick as present? **M** share per band against the references' minimum, median and maximum. **Fix** [level-matched reference](techniques/mixing/alex-rome-level-matched-reference-ab.md).
47. Are the remaining small, broad tonal gaps closed? **M** tonal rows inside the references' range. **Fix** on the groups first, else [two master shelves](techniques/mixing/alex-rome-c-master-eq-from-the-reference-curve.md).

## 7. Details
48. Is there ear candy in the gaps, never on top of the riff or the words, and never the same trick twice running? **M** candy onsets on steps where the riff is silent; no overlap with sung words (`transcribe`). **Fix** [ear-candy pass](techniques/arrangement/ryos-b-ear-candy-pass-after-the-core.md), [delay throw](techniques/mixing/ryos-b-delay-throw-on-one-word.md).
49. If there is a vocal, is a piece of it in the drop? **M** its stem is present there and sits under the lead at 1 to 4 kHz. **Fix** [washed vocal shot](techniques/sound-design/ryos-b-washed-vocal-shot-for-the-drop.md).
50. Do fills reuse the track's own sounds, and are the effects sized to the genre? **M** by ear only. **Fix** [transition details](techniques/arrangement/ryos-transition-details-reverses-cuts-risers.md).
51. Do leads and basses have small pitch or filter moves at phrase ends? **M** pitch reading at the phrase end on a single-voice stem. **Fix** [pitch-bend dives](techniques/sound-design/ryos-b-pitch-bend-dives-on-the-lead.md), [pitch-bend ramps](techniques/bass/moonboy-b-pitch-bend-ramps-on-the-bassline.md).
52. Last pass: a full bounce against the references. **M** every row that is outside their range is listed, and the list is shorter than last time. Say plainly what has not been judged by ear. **Fix** [colour first, refine second](techniques/mindset/ryos-colour-first-refine-second.md).

## What this list cannot tell you
None of the creators gave a loudness target, a limiter setting or a section-length plan for tech house or bass
house, so lines 19, 27 and 45 lean on the reference shelf, not on teaching. Lines marked "by ear only" (2, 11,
27, 50) are Rohil's to answer.
