# Tools the creators use, and what Rohil has

Taken from the "Tools the creator uses" section of the 27 source digests and sorted by the number of videos in
which the tool appears. "Videos" counts a video when the tool is used, shown on a channel strip, or recommended in
it; a mention that only translates to Rohil's set-up is not counted. Three of Moonboy's eight videos are chapters
cut from his full tech house tutorial, so his counts include the same footage twice. Ryos works in FL Studio,
Moonboy in Ableton Live, Alex Rome in Logic Pro (six videos), Ableton Live (five) or both.

"Rohil" says whether he owns it, or the nearest thing he owns and what is lost. His list (from
[README.md](README.md)): Logic Pro 11, Serum 2, Vital, Kickstart 2, Trackspacer 2.5, OTT, Valhalla Supermassive,
Mastering The Mix (BASSROOM, MIXROOM, LEVELS, LIMITER, REFERENCE, RESO, STEREOVAULT, FUSER, ANIMATE,
FASTERMASTER), Ozone Imager 2, Splice, and Logic's own plug-ins. The equivalents are the editor's suggestions and
are untested; where a Logic plug-in is named that is not on the README's list it is marked "Logic stock".

## What this means for Rohil
- He already owns the tools used most for the jobs that matter: Serum 2, OTT, Kickstart 2, REFERENCE, Ozone
  Imager 2, Supermassive, Trackspacer, Splice. Alex Rome's whole four-pass mix uses only stock EQ plus three
  plug-ins he has.
- The gaps that change what can be done: a dynamic EQ band (RESO covers resonances, not one hand-set band), a
  drawn volume shaper (LFOTool, ShaperBox), a hard clipper for the master, per-band phase limiting, and Ryos's
  preset romplers and own sample packs, from which most of his sounds come.
- Kilohearts Trance Gate, Voxengo MSED and Cymatics Origin are free; whether to install them is Rohil's call.
- Related cards: [maintenance chain](techniques/workflow/ryos-c-maintenance-chain-on-every-track.md) (the
  utility set Ryos ends every chain with), [four-pass mix](techniques/mixing/alex-rome-c-four-pass-mix-eq-ott-sidechain-reference.md),
  [keep wide layers in phase](techniques/mixing/ryos-c-keep-wide-layers-in-phase.md). Every card's "In Rohil's
  setup" section gives its own translation.

## What to buy, and what not to (the copilot's reading of the table, 2026-11-20)

This part is a recommendation, not the creators' teaching. Check current prices before buying; none were looked up.

Rohil already owns the tools that carry these 27 videos: Serum (22 videos), OTT (13), Kickstart 2 (9), an EQ with an
analyzer (Logic's Channel EQ), Splice. Nothing below is needed to finish a track. In order of how much it would help:

1. **ValhallaVintageVerb.** The one effect both Alex Rome (7 videos) and Ryos (2) reach for on leads, plucks, pianos
   and vocals, and it is inexpensive. Until then: ChromaVerb with a low cut near 500 Hz and a high cut near 9 kHz on
   the return (see "reverb" in INDEX.md).
2. **A clipper** (Ryos names StandardCLIP, "or any clipper"). His way of working is to produce into a clipper with
   the kick at zero, and the one thing Rohil's E85 mix lacked was a master chain. Until then: Logic's Limiter on Stereo
   Out, which is what version 1 of the copy uses.
3. **FabFilter Pro-Q**, later. Ryos uses it in every video for narrow cuts, side-only cuts and one dynamic band.
   Channel EQ does the first two (it has a Mid/Side mode); buy Pro-Q when a dynamic band is what is missing, not before.
4. **A formant and pitch tool for vocals** (Moonboy names Little AlterBoy), only if Logic's Vocal Transformer and
   Pitch Correction turn out not to be enough for tech house vocal tricks. Try those first.
5. **Drum samples made for the genre.** Moonboy's free house drum kit and Project X pack for tech house; Ryos's packs
   when progressive house starts. "The pro sound is chosen, not mixed" is the first principle in PRINCIPLES.md, and
   it is mostly about samples.

Not worth buying now: another synth (Nexus, Spire, Sylenth1, Diva: Serum 2 and Vital cover what is taught), more
distortion or saturation (ChromaGlow, Overdrive and Phat FX are in Logic), ShaperBox or LFOTool (Kickstart 2 and
Logic's Tremolo cover the uses shown), soothe2 (RESO is the nearest thing he has), a mastering suite (he has one).
