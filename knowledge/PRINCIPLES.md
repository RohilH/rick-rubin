# Principles: what the three creators agree on

Themes that recur across Ryos (7 videos), Moonboy (8, three of them chapters of one tutorial) and Alex Rome (12),
most important first for someone early on. "Who" counts the videos where the creator says or shows it. "Check" is
what the copilot can measure (loudness and crest, energy per band, side % per band, level per element over time,
the 16 references); it cannot hear, so anything about how it sounds also needs a bounce for Rohil.
All cards: [INDEX.md](INDEX.md). The checklist: [FINISHED.md](FINISHED.md).

## 1. The "pro sound" is chosen, not mixed
The right sample and patch for the genre and mood do more than any processing: the same notes on the right
sound are a different track. Replace a wrong sound; do not repair it.
- **Who:** Alex Rome 3 (pro sound, weak bass, house songs); Ryos 3 (feedback stream: 6 of 12 tracks had a sound
  that did not fit; bass house; breakdowns); Moonboy 1 (sample quality first).
- **Cards:** [pro-sound audit](techniques/workflow/alex-rome-c-pro-sound-audit-lead-chords-bass-drums.md),
  [kick that fits the genre](techniques/drums/ryos-kick-that-fits-the-genre.md),
  [kick with a full tail](techniques/drums/alex-rome-c-kick-with-a-full-tail-half-a-beat-long.md).
- **Check:** partly. Kick length and low-band energy late in the sample; tonal balance per band against the
  genre's references. Whether a sound fits the mood is by ear only.

## 2. Few parts, simple notes; one or two sounds carry the idea
The effort goes into sound, rhythm and arrangement, not into more notes or more tracks. If the main one or two
sounds do not state the mood alone, layers will not create it. Only one part gets to be busy.
- **Who:** Ryos 4 (breakdowns, bass house, feedback: 6 of 12 needed simpler notes, EP 51); Moonboy 2 (three
  elements; a riff from two notes); Alex Rome 3 (two-note chords, simpler chords, one chord as a progression).
- **Cards:** [two sounds must carry the feeling](techniques/mindset/ryos-b-two-sounds-must-carry-the-feeling.md),
  [one fast part](techniques/arrangement/ryos-c-one-fast-part-everything-else-slower.md),
  [two-note riff](techniques/melody/moonboy-b-two-note-techno-riff-with-octave-drops.md).
- **Check:** onsets per bar for each element from its level over time: one element clearly ahead, no two with
  different accent patterns in the same bar. A bounce of the two core sounds alone for Rohil.

## 3. One progression and one rhythm feed every part
Derive, do not invent: the bass is the chords' bottom notes, the lead their top notes, the drop reuses the
break's harmony, and bass, stabs and fills copy the lead's rhythm. That is what makes sections sound like one song.
- **Who:** Alex Rome 6 (house songs, flip, layering, arrangement, trench, weak bass); Ryos 3 (breakdowns, EP 50,
  subscriber fix); Moonboy 2 (melodic house, bass house).
- **Cards:** [one progression feeds every part](techniques/harmony/moonboy-b-one-progression-feeds-every-part.md),
  [one progression for break and drop](techniques/harmony/ryos-b-one-progression-for-break-and-drop.md),
  [bassline from the chords](techniques/bass/alex-rome-b-bassline-from-the-chords-bottom-notes.md),
  [bass copies the melody's rhythm](techniques/bass/ryos-pg-bass-copies-melody-rhythm-with-slides.md).
- **Check:** `notes` on bounced stems: bass roots equal chord roots, in the same order in break and drop; onsets
  of bass and lead coincide where the rhythm was copied.

## 4. Decide who is in front, then make room for it
Name the loudest, second and third element of each section. The star (the lead in progressive house, the vocal
or bass in tech house) gets brightness, body near 300 Hz and width; everything else gives way.
- **Who:** Ryos 3 (feedback: the lead was not in front in 10 of 12 tracks; KSHMR remix; breakdowns); Alex Rome 3
  (weak bass, mixing tips, mixing in four passes: both references showed his bass louder than the lead).
- **Cards:** [clear 200 to 500 Hz](techniques/mixing/ryos-clear-200-500hz-pocket-for-the-lead.md),
  [one melody in front](techniques/mixing/alex-rome-c-one-melody-in-front-before-adding-bass.md),
  [centre anchor, then widen](techniques/sound-design/ryos-lead-stack-centre-anchor-then-widen.md).
- **Check:** level of lead, bass and chord stems over the drop; energy in 200 to 500 Hz per stem (the star
  highest); the same balance in the references.

## 5. Kick and bass take turns, and one layer owns the sub
The listener should feel the kick, then the bass. One source sits at 40 to 80 Hz; every other part is cut out
of it; three low things on one beat means one is deleted.
- **Who:** Alex Rome 6 (weak bass, both mixing videos, layering, pro sound, flip); Ryos 4 (feedback: 8 of 12
  tracks, subscriber fix, EP 51, bass house); Moonboy 3 (offbeat bassline, low roots, the ducked sub).
- **Cards:** [bass octave at 40 to 80 Hz](techniques/bass/alex-rome-c-bass-octave-by-the-analyzer-40-80hz.md),
  [bass in layers](techniques/bass/ryos-bass-in-layers-sub-harmonics-mid.md),
  [sub duck traced from the kick](techniques/mixing/ryos-c-sub-duck-curve-traced-from-the-kick.md),
  [offbeat bassline](techniques/bass/moonboy-offbeat-bassline-and-its-variations.md).
- **Check:** strongest bass peak per note between 40 and 80 Hz; bass level per 16th lowest on the kick's steps
  (pump inside the references' range); side % under 150 Hz near zero; harmonics above the sub with no gap.

## 6. Something the listener can name changes every eight bars
Add, remove, open or close something at each eight-bar boundary; the second half of a drop has more than the
first. A kick alone does not count; two changes per boundary is a good budget.
- **Who:** Ryos 3 (feedback: 4 of 12, KSHMR remix, bass house); Alex Rome 3 (two-loop rule, house songs, flip);
  Moonboy 2 (sections in eights with a lift at bar five; A and B drop halves).
- **Cards:** [every eight bars](techniques/arrangement/ryos-something-significant-every-eight-bars.md),
  [two-loop rule](techniques/arrangement/alex-rome-b-two-loop-rule.md),
  [second-pass variations](techniques/arrangement/ryos-b-second-pass-variations-in-a-drop.md).
- **Check:** which elements start or stop at each boundary; band energy per eight-bar block (top end stepping
  up); share of near-identical two-bar blocks and texture changes per minute against the references.

## 7. A song is its transitions
A loop or a drop is a section. The skill is the join: filter things away, preview the next section, hand one
lead to the next, and play across every boundary before writing on.
- **Who:** Alex Rome 3 (flip, arrangement, trench); Ryos 4 (feedback: 6 of 12 lacked transition detail; EP 50 and
  51 builds; KSHMR: filtering is "critical"); Moonboy 1 (band-pass fill, his only transition device).
- **Cards:** [build by filtering](techniques/arrangement/ryos-pg-build-by-filtering-everything-away.md),
  [opposite automation](techniques/arrangement/alex-rome-b-opposite-automation-handoff.md),
  [transition details](techniques/arrangement/ryos-transition-details-reverses-cuts-risers.md).
- **Check:** across a build, low-band energy falls and brightness rises bar by bar, with a clear step in level
  and low end at the drop; the size of that step against the references. "Satisfying" is by ear.

## 8. Every layer has a job, and it is the opposite of its neighbour
Layer for a missing register, attack, width or character; never ten of the same saw. A layer that changes
nothing when muted is deleted.
- **Who:** Ryos 5 (feedback, EP 50, EP 51, bass house, subscriber fix); Alex Rome 2 (layering, pro sound);
  Moonboy 2 (layers by register; groove bass under a character bass).
- **Cards:** [layer by contrast](techniques/sound-design/ryos-layer-by-contrast.md),
  [lead stack in six roles](techniques/sound-design/ryos-pg-lead-stack-six-roles.md),
  [four-layer bass](techniques/bass/ryos-pg-four-layer-bass-stack.md).
- **Check:** energy per band for each layer's stem: each peaks in a different band; muting one moves a band.

## 9. Fill what is between and behind the main parts
"Finished but thin" is usually a missing mid-range driver, atmosphere bed, top-end sparkle, or silence between
notes. Fill them with parts nobody would name: a bed, a quiet percussion loop, delay tails.
- **Who:** Ryos 4 (feedback: 5 of 12 had too few layers; KSHMR remix; EP 50; EP 51); Alex Rome 2 (layering,
  pro sound); Moonboy 2 (vocal pads in the vocal and bass house videos).
- **Cards:** [fill the mid range](techniques/arrangement/ryos-fill-the-mid-range-and-atmosphere-bed.md),
  [hidden percussion loop](techniques/sound-design/alex-rome-c-hidden-percussion-loop-under-the-music.md),
  [pluck lead with delay](techniques/sound-design/alex-rome-c-pluck-lead-with-delay-to-fill-the-gaps.md).
- **Check:** no dip between about 300 Hz and 2 kHz in the section's spectrum; energy above 8 kHz in breaks; the
  lead's level between notes not falling to silence; the section does not shrink when the vocal stem is muted.

## 10. Everything keeps moving
Static sounds read as amateur. Give each part one moving thing: an envelope that decays, a filter that opens
over the section, a volume shape, a pitch bend. Design sounds closed so there is room to open them.
- **Who:** Alex Rome 4 (flip, pro sound, sound design, trench); Ryos 4 (KSHMR remix, EP 50, subscriber fix, bass
  house); Moonboy 3 (wave, filter, movement; vocal tricks; the shared bus).
- **Cards:** [chords that decay](techniques/sound-design/alex-rome-c-chords-that-decay-like-a-piano.md),
  [wavetable, filter, movement](techniques/sound-design/moonboy-wavetable-filter-movement.md),
  [maintenance chain](techniques/workflow/ryos-c-maintenance-chain-on-every-track.md).
- **Check:** each sustained stem's level and brightness over time is not a flat line; `serum describe` reports
  movement; the references' movement figure.

## 11. The drop is the break made harder, by sound and rhythm
Energy comes from sound choice, note length and rhythm on the same notes: the pad becomes a pluck, held notes
get shorter, soft layers get distorted or replaced. The break keeps its soft versions.
- **Who:** Ryos 2 (EP 51, subscriber fix); Alex Rome 2 (house songs, layering); Moonboy 2 (melodic house: two
  drops on one harmony; bass house: the trance melody's notes in heavy sounds).
- **Cards:** [harden break sounds](techniques/arrangement/ryos-c-harden-break-sounds-for-the-drop.md),
  [one progression, two energies](techniques/harmony/alex-rome-b-one-progression-at-two-energy-levels.md),
  [bouncy drop](techniques/bass/ryos-b-bouncy-drop-short-notes-octave-jumps-faster-changes.md).
- **Check:** drop against break: louder, lower crest, more high-mid and top energy, more onsets per bar.

## 12. Leave room: what is not heard still costs headroom
Low-cut everything but kick and bass, thin supporting sounds to their one band, filter reverbs, and treat the top
end as a budget. Set each cut in context, not in solo.
- **Who:** Alex Rome 2 (both mixing videos); Ryos 3 (KSHMR remix, EP 50, feedback: 5 of 12 crowded 200 to
  500 Hz); Moonboy 1 (chords high-passed before the bass layer goes in).
- **Cards:** [low-cut everything](techniques/mixing/alex-rome-low-cut-everything-but-kick-and-bass.md),
  [low cut against the bass](techniques/mixing/alex-rome-c-find-the-low-cut-against-the-bass.md),
  [low mids set distance](techniques/mixing/ryos-c-low-mids-set-distance-cut-what-is-masked.md).
- **Check:** energy under the cut per stem; low-mid share of the mix against the references; crest rising at the
  same loudness after the cuts.

## 13. Work at release level and compare at matched loudness
Keep a clipper on the master from the first bar with the kick at 0 dB, and A/B against a released track of the
same genre with the level matched: brightness, bass level, kick level.
- **Who:** Alex Rome 3 (both mixing videos, weak bass); Ryos 1 (feedback: 3 of 12 arrived too quiet or crushed);
  Moonboy 1 (soft clip on the drum group).
- **Cards:** [clipper, kick at zero](techniques/mastering/ryos-produce-into-a-clipper-kick-at-zero.md),
  [level-matched reference](techniques/mixing/alex-rome-level-matched-reference-ab.md),
  [master EQ from the reference](techniques/mixing/alex-rome-c-master-eq-from-the-reference-curve.md).
- **Check:** fully. Integrated loudness, crest and share per band against the 16 references; no clipped samples.

## 14. Width is managed, and the centre comes first
Wide sounds seem louder than they are and fall apart in mono. Build a thick centre layer before widening, keep
the low end mono, narrow lows more than highs, and use width as a contrast in time.
- **Who:** Ryos 2 (feedback, KSHMR remix); Alex Rome 3 (layering, mixing tips, sound design); Moonboy 1 (the
  quiet rides are the only wide drum).
- **Cards:** [keep wide layers in phase](techniques/mixing/ryos-c-keep-wide-layers-in-phase.md),
  [balance in mono](techniques/mixing/alex-rome-balance-in-mono.md),
  [narrow pad, then wide](techniques/sound-design/alex-rome-c-narrow-pad-then-wide-layer-on-the-phrase.md).
- **Check:** fully. Side % per band (near zero under 150 Hz, rising with frequency), correlation at or above zero
  per stem, mono loss, against the references.

## 15. Even out dynamics by role, not everywhere
Melodic parts that are too dynamic vanish over a bass; a little OTT glues, a lot on everything is tiring. Most
on the star, a touch on the rest, none on the sub; clip drum peaks instead of compressing them.
- **Who:** Alex Rome 2 (mixing in four passes, pro sound); Ryos 3 (feedback: 5 of 12; breakdowns; EP 50 as the
  counter-case); Moonboy 2 (drum group and clap; OTT last on vocals).
- **Cards:** [OTT by role](techniques/mixing/alex-rome-c-ott-amount-by-role.md),
  [slam melodic groups](techniques/mixing/ryos-slam-melodic-groups-with-compression.md),
  [drum group soft clip](techniques/mixing/moonboy-drum-group-soft-clip.md).
- **Check:** crest per stem before and after; the kick still the loudest step in the drum group; mix crest
  against the references.

## 16. The vocal is an instrument, and the track's own vocal is material
Tune it, gate it, wash it, vocode it; cut one word for the drop, a stutter for ear candy, a pad from its reverb.
- **Who:** Moonboy 5 (tech house full and vocal chapter, vocal tricks, bass house, melodic house); Ryos 3
  (subscriber fix, KSHMR remix, breakdowns); Alex Rome 3 (flip, melody styles, arrangement).
- **Cards:** [vocoder chain](techniques/sound-design/moonboy-vocoder-vocal-chain.md),
  [washed vocal shot](techniques/sound-design/ryos-b-washed-vocal-shot-for-the-drop.md),
  [vocal phrase to stutter lead](techniques/sound-design/alex-rome-b-vocal-phrase-to-stutter-lead.md).
- **Check:** `notes` for key; `transcribe` that words survive and candy does not overlap sung words; side % and
  low cut on the washes.

## 17. Work in stages and test in context
Foundation first (vocal and chords, or the melody), then each addition checked against the rest; build the drop
before the build; write two versions and choose; colour first, refine later; mix on another day.
- **Who:** Alex Rome 6 (trench, flip, weak bass, melody styles, arrangement, mixing tips); Ryos 5 (EP 50, EP 51,
  bass house, breakdowns, feedback); Moonboy 1 (a borrowed vocal as a placeholder).
- **Cards:** [trench method](techniques/workflow/alex-rome-b-trench-method.md),
  [two basses, then choose](techniques/bass/alex-rome-c-two-basses-sustained-and-rhythmic-then-choose.md),
  [colour first, refine second](techniques/mindset/ryos-colour-first-refine-second.md).
- **Check:** not by measurement. The record is the check: a bounce and a `verdict` per added part and per join.

## Where the creators disagree or differ
- **A hidden bed's level.** Alex Rome hides his drone and percussion loop so nobody notices them; Ryos says an
  atmosphere nobody can hear is wasted and turns it up. Try both.
- **Clap and kick.** Moonboy starts the clap a few ms early for depth; Ryos shaves the clap's transient so only
  the kick hits. Alex Rome wants a kick with a full body for half a beat; Ryos trims the progressive kick's tail.
- **Sidechain.** Alex Rome: one Kickstart on the instrument group, keyed by the kick, for separation first and
  pump as a genre choice. Ryos: a shaper last on each part, depth automated by section, the sub's curve drawn
  from the kick. Moonboy: an offbeat bass that avoids the kick, and one "Sidechain" bus for synths and basses.
- **Reverb.** Alex Rome filters every reverb (500 Hz to about 9 kHz). Ryos and Moonboy also use bright, nearly
  wet reverbs on inserts as the sound itself; for a lead Ryos moves the reverb to a send with pre-delay.
- **Compressing leads.** Ryos tells senders to slam weak pianos and lead groups, yet keeps his own big lead
  stack dynamic with "just a taste". Alex Rome puts the most OTT on the lead. It depends on the source.
- **Chord size.** Alex Rome: a bottom note plus a third, no fifth. Ryos: root, fifth, octave, tenth, and no
  third at all for dark music. Moonboy: root, fifth, tenth. All three spread the chord and watch the top note.
- **Supersaws.** Alex Rome calls the supersaw lead generic and prefers a pluck or a vocal stutter; Ryos and
  Moonboy build big saw and string stacks. That is genre: festival and big room against melodic house.
- **Where to start.** Moonboy: drums. Alex Rome: vocal and chords. Ryos: a piano over a drone, a banked melody,
  or (bass house) the sound itself. Bass rhythm: offbeat (Moonboy), the chords' rhythm (Alex Rome), the melody's
  rhythm or one held note (Ryos). Tech house tempo: Moonboy used 128 in 2023 and 130 in 2025.

## What none of them covered
- **Mastering.** One card. No limiter settings, loudness target or final chain; Ryos keeps it for a paid course.
- **A whole tech house or bass house arrangement.** Moonboy stops at a loop or a drop: no DJ intro and outro,
  no breakdown or build design, no second half. Full bar maps exist only for Ryos's progressive house.
- **Swing amounts** (a swing setting is visible twice, never discussed), hat and percussion programming past
  ghost hits, and how a track ends.
- **Compressor basics** (attack, release, ratio), gain staging, bus routing, sends and returns, panning.
- **Mixing a vocal**: EQ, de-essing, level against the bass, cleaning an acapella. Only effects are taught.
- **Kick tuning**, the kick's key against the bass, choosing a key (beyond Moonboy's low D or D sharp).
- **Making risers, impacts and fills**: all three use samples. Small-speaker checks beyond one remark on subs.
- **Logic.** Ryos and Moonboy never use it. Every translation to Logic and Rohil's plug-ins on the cards is
  untested, and nothing in this knowledge base has been judged by ear.
