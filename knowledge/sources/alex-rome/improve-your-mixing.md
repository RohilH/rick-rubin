## What it is about
He takes a student's unmixed piano house drop (piano chords, arps, a sub, a bass layer, drums) and mixes it in four
passes, in the order he gives every student: low cuts, a little OTT, sidechain to the kick, then a reference track.
It is done in Ableton Live with stock EQ and three plug-ins Rohil owns (OTT, Kickstart 2, REFERENCE). Nothing else
is used: no ordinary compressor, no saturation, no bus processing beyond OTT on the drum group. The value is the
order and the reasons, which make a rough balance usable quickly.

## The main ideas
1. **Clarity starts in the bass.** The test for a clear mix is whether you can follow the sub the whole time; if it
   pops in and out, something else is sitting on it ([5:33](https://youtu.be/XrGZPWuiJ7o?t=333),
   [6:00](https://youtu.be/XrGZPWuiJ7o?t=360)).
2. **EQ against the thing you are making room for, not in solo.** He sets each low cut while the bass plays and
   looks for the point where both still sound full ([2:15](https://youtu.be/XrGZPWuiJ7o?t=135)).
3. **Low end you cannot hear still costs headroom**, so he cuts it from high parts too
   ([3:47](https://youtu.be/XrGZPWuiJ7o?t=227)).
4. **OTT is a strength and glue dial, used by role.** More on the lead, a touch on secondary parts and on the drum
   group, none on the sub; a lot on everything is unlistenable ([7:38](https://youtu.be/XrGZPWuiJ7o?t=458)).
5. **Whatever comes before OTT gets crushed into the sound**, so its place relative to reverb and delay is a choice
   ([8:45](https://youtu.be/XrGZPWuiJ7o?t=525)).
6. **Sidechain exists to keep kick and bass apart.** The pumping effect is a genre choice on top of that
   ([12:55](https://youtu.be/XrGZPWuiJ7o?t=775)).
7. **The ear gets you most of the way; a reference gets the rest.** Compare brightness, levels and kick, and ignore
   that the reference has more parts ([15:20](https://youtu.be/XrGZPWuiJ7o?t=920),
   [18:28](https://youtu.be/XrGZPWuiJ7o?t=1108)).

## Step by step
1. [1:04](https://youtu.be/XrGZPWuiJ7o?t=64) Low cut on every instrument except kick, bass and drums, "up to around
   150 Hz" (said). On the piano chords, shown at [1:58](https://youtu.be/XrGZPWuiJ7o?t=118): EQ Eight band 1 as a
   low cut at 255 Hz, Q 0.82, mid-sweep; settled at [3:32](https://youtu.be/XrGZPWuiJ7o?t=212): 278 Hz, Q 0.69.
2. [2:15](https://youtu.be/XrGZPWuiJ7o?t=135) He plays bass and piano together and moves the cut: too high and the
   piano thins, too low and the sub struggles.
3. [3:26](https://youtu.be/XrGZPWuiJ7o?t=206) Arps: low end shows on the analyzer though he cannot hear it; cut
   anyway. The arp chain at [9:10](https://youtu.be/XrGZPWuiJ7o?t=550) shows its EQ Eight low cut at 400 Hz.
4. [4:50](https://youtu.be/XrGZPWuiJ7o?t=290) Sub: no EQ. Bass layer: treated like any instrument and cut away
   from the sub. Shown at [5:18](https://youtu.be/XrGZPWuiJ7o?t=318): a 48 dB/oct low cut at 352 Hz, Q 0.72.
5. [6:13](https://youtu.be/XrGZPWuiJ7o?t=373) OTT. The window at [7:05](https://youtu.be/XrGZPWuiJ7o?t=425) starts
   at Depth 0 %, Time 100 %, both strengths 100 %; he turns Depth up on the piano. How far it ends is not shown.
6. [8:45](https://youtu.be/XrGZPWuiJ7o?t=525) Order. Arp chain shown at [9:10](https://youtu.be/XrGZPWuiJ7o?t=550):
   Serum, OTT, ValhallaVintageVerb, Ozone Imager, EQ Eight. He moves OTT after the reverb and back to compare.
7. [10:07](https://youtu.be/XrGZPWuiJ7o?t=607) All drums including the kick in one group, OTT on the group. Demo at
   Depth 80 % ([10:18](https://youtu.be/XrGZPWuiJ7o?t=618)), kept at 14 %
   ([10:45](https://youtu.be/XrGZPWuiJ7o?t=645)).
8. [11:10](https://youtu.be/XrGZPWuiJ7o?t=670) Sidechain with Kickstart 2 on the instrument group, triggered by
   the kick's audio (said). Shown at [11:56](https://youtu.be/XrGZPWuiJ7o?t=716): 1/4, AUDIO, Mix 100 %, first
   curve, the kick's waveform drawn behind the curve. Bass, chords and melody all duck.
9. [13:57](https://youtu.be/XrGZPWuiJ7o?t=837) REFERENCE 2 on the master with Kygo and HAYLA's "Without You"
   loaded (same genre, similar bassline, piano lead). Shown at [14:55](https://youtu.be/XrGZPWuiJ7o?t=895): his mix
   −13.8 LUFS integrated, the reference −6.9; the tonal curve asks for about 4 dB less near 100 Hz and up to 10 dB
   more above 5 kHz.
10. [16:25](https://youtu.be/XrGZPWuiJ7o?t=985) What he hears: his leads are duller, his bass is louder.
11. [17:27](https://youtu.be/XrGZPWuiJ7o?t=1047) Fix in the mix (more OTT, EQ, faders) or, when the gaps are small,
    with one EQ on the master. Shown at [17:58](https://youtu.be/XrGZPWuiJ7o?t=1078): low shelf at 86.1 Hz, −5.08 dB,
    Q 0.71, and a shelf lifting everything above about 1 kHz by roughly 2.5 dB (read off the curve). After it
    ([18:45](https://youtu.be/XrGZPWuiJ7o?t=1125)) the tonal curve stays within about 2 dB.

## Tools the creator uses
- Ableton Live: EQ Eight (low cuts, master shelves), track groups.
- Xfer OTT (free): strength and glue on instruments and the drum group. Rohil owns it.
- Nicky Romero Kickstart 2: ducking, triggered by the kick's audio. Rohil owns it.
- Mastering The Mix REFERENCE 2: level-matched A/B and tonal curve. Rohil owns it.
- Seen in chains, not discussed: Serum, Kontakt, ValhallaVintageVerb, Ozone Imager.
- A free PDF mixing guide (link in the video description; not read).

## Not covered or unclear
- The 150 Hz figure is said; every cut shown is higher (278 to 400 Hz). He gives no rule per instrument.
- Final OTT depths on the piano and arps are not on screen; only the drum group's 14 % is.
- Kickstart's settings after he "finds a setting" on the group and on the bass are not in a captured frame
  (the frames at 12:45 and 13:45 are of him talking).
- The second shelf's exact frequency and gain on the master EQ are not readable, only the curve.
- No compressor other than OTT, no saturation, no panning or width, no gain staging, no mastering.
- "126 %" is a title, not a measurement.
