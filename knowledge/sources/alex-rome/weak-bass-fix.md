## What it is about
A student's deep house track (126 BPM, G# minor by the vocal sample's file name) has good chords and melodies but
no bassline and a kick that disappears. In Logic Pro he first tidies the mix (new kick, one melody in front), then
writes two different basses from the same chord roots, a sustained one and a rhythmic one, and tests each against
the rest of the track before choosing. Both sounds come from Serum's init patch. It is the closest of the five
videos to Rohil's situation: a track with good ideas that does not yet sound finished.

## The main ideas
1. **Do not add bass to a messy mix.** Fix the kick and the balance first, so you can hear what the bass does
   ([1:46](https://youtu.be/HLCi1uYq8r0?t=106)).
2. **A kick is judged by its tail.** Two kicks at the same meter level: the one whose body tapers away is
   inaudible in the mix, the round one is not ([2:07](https://youtu.be/HLCi1uYq8r0?t=127)).
3. **A bassline starts as the roots of the chords, in the octave where the analyzer peaks at 40 to 80 Hz**
   ([4:44](https://youtu.be/HLCi1uYq8r0?t=284), [6:08](https://youtu.be/HLCi1uYq8r0?t=368)).
4. **Sidechain does two jobs on a held bass**: it keeps the kick clear and it supplies the movement the held note
   lacks ([7:51](https://youtu.be/HLCi1uYq8r0?t=471)).
5. **A rhythm needs a sound with a shape.** The flat sub cannot show a dotted-eighth pattern, so the rhythmic bass
   gets a filter-envelope pluck ([10:14](https://youtu.be/HLCi1uYq8r0?t=614)).
6. **Write two, test in stages, then choose.** Drums only, then one lead at a time, then the transition into the
   section, then against a released track ([11:17](https://youtu.be/HLCi1uYq8r0?t=677),
   [12:41](https://youtu.be/HLCi1uYq8r0?t=761)).

## Step by step
1. [2:07](https://youtu.be/HLCi1uYq8r0?t=127) The kick. Original sample shown at
   [2:18](https://youtu.be/HLCi1uYq8r0?t=138): loud front, then a body that shrinks steadily. He replaces it rather
   than process it. New sample shown at [2:48](https://youtu.be/HLCi1uYq8r0?t=168): full height for most of its
   length, about half a beat long (said).
2. [3:31](https://youtu.be/HLCi1uYq8r0?t=211) Four melodies play at once. He rebalances off camera and keeps the
   one he likes most in front ([4:18](https://youtu.be/HLCi1uYq8r0?t=258)).
3. [4:44](https://youtu.be/HLCi1uYq8r0?t=284) Sustained bass: one root per chord (G#, B, F#, C#; said), taken by
   copying the chord region and keeping the roots (shown [5:12](https://youtu.be/HLCi1uYq8r0?t=312)).
4. [5:26](https://youtu.be/HLCi1uYq8r0?t=326) Sound: Serum init saw, filter on, MG Low 24, cutoff pulled low (shown
   [5:38](https://youtu.be/HLCi1uYq8r0?t=338)). Drop it by octaves until the strongest peak is at 40 to 80 Hz
   (shown [6:18](https://youtu.be/HLCi1uYq8r0?t=378) on Channel EQ: fundamental just above 50 Hz, harmonics stepping
   down above it).
5. [6:48](https://youtu.be/HLCi1uYq8r0?t=408) The melodies need no EQ: they already leave the low end empty.
6. [7:29](https://youtu.be/HLCi1uYq8r0?t=449) Kickstart 2 on the bass. Shown at
   [7:38](https://youtu.be/HLCi1uYq8r0?t=458): 1/4, SYNC, second curve, Mix 100 %; at
   [8:25](https://youtu.be/HLCi1uYq8r0?t=505): Mix 88 %, strip order Serum, Channel EQ, Kickstart. He tries a long
   pump and a quick one.
7. [9:11](https://youtu.be/HLCi1uYq8r0?t=551) Rhythmic bass: same roots, in the rhythm of the busiest melody,
   dotted eighths that restart every half bar (said). First note shown at
   [9:48](https://youtu.be/HLCi1uYq8r0?t=588): G#0 in Logic's naming, three sixteenths long.
8. [10:14](https://youtu.be/HLCi1uYq8r0?t=614) New sound: ENV 2 on the cutoff (shown
   [10:42](https://youtu.be/HLCi1uYq8r0?t=642)), sustain 0, decay lengthened to 2.70 s so notes run into each other
   (shown [11:08](https://youtu.be/HLCi1uYq8r0?t=668)). The SUB oscillator is switched on in those frames; he does
   not mention it. Sidechained as before.
9. [11:17](https://youtu.be/HLCi1uYq8r0?t=677) Tests: with drums, with each lead, with the vocal chop, then from
   the bars before the section ([12:20](https://youtu.be/HLCi1uYq8r0?t=740)). He prefers the rhythmic bass, A/Bs
   against a released track ([12:55](https://youtu.be/HLCi1uYq8r0?t=775)), then replays the sustained one and
   likes it too.

## Tools the creator uses
- Logic Pro: Channel EQ analyzer (bass octave), piano roll, region copying.
- Serum 1 (the student's version): both basses from the init patch.
- Nicky Romero Kickstart 2: ducking the bass, tempo-synced. Rohil owns it.
- REFERENCE on the Stereo Out (seen on the channel strip at 8:25) for the A/B with a released track.
- Splice samples in the student's project (kick, top loop, shaker loop, vocal hook; names on screen).
