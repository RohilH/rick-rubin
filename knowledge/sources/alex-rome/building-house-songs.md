## What it is about
A song skeleton for house in Ableton Live with Serum: write one chord progression in two forms, a low-energy pad
version and a high-energy pluck version, give each form a 16-bar block, and dress each block with instruments of
matching energy. Something new enters halfway through each block. The demo is a deep, melodic house idea of 32
bars: a relaxed section where a vocal verse would go, then pluck chords alone, then bass and drums. It is the
oldest of the four videos and the least complete as a system, but the clearest on how sound and rhythm, not
notes, set energy.

## The main ideas
1. **One progression, two energies.** The notes stay; sound choice and rhythm are what raise the energy
   ([0:21](https://youtu.be/kSXHTJJ7D34?t=21), [2:57](https://youtu.be/kSXHTJJ7D34?t=177)).
2. **Sections are sorted by energy.** Relaxed instruments go with the relaxed chords, drums and bass with the
   energetic ones; that sorting is the arrangement ([4:44](https://youtu.be/kSXHTJJ7D34?t=284)).
3. **Two notes are enough.** A bass note and a third: intervals, not full chords, and mellow because of it
   ([2:36](https://youtu.be/kSXHTJJ7D34?t=156)).
4. **Something enters halfway through every 16 bars** (his "eight-bar rule", from another of his videos)
   ([7:30](https://youtu.be/kSXHTJJ7D34?t=450), [9:57](https://youtu.be/kSXHTJJ7D34?t=597)).
5. **Contrast tight with loose.** A stiff pad next to a slow, wide, drenched one sounds deep
   ([6:29](https://youtu.be/kSXHTJJ7D34?t=389)).
6. **The bass takes the rhythm of the part above it,** with small gaps between notes so the rhythm reads
   ([8:12](https://youtu.be/kSXHTJJ7D34?t=492), [8:54](https://youtu.be/kSXHTJJ7D34?t=534)).
7. **Groove hides in small things:** layered hats, a closed hat off the grid, a percussion loop nobody is meant
   to notice ([9:14](https://youtu.be/kSXHTJJ7D34?t=554), [9:35](https://youtu.be/kSXHTJJ7D34?t=575)).
8. **Pick each sound for the mood:** muffled kick and darker clap for a deeper track
   ([9:14](https://youtu.be/kSXHTJJ7D34?t=554)).

## Step by step
1. [0:30](https://youtu.be/kSXHTJJ7D34?t=30) Low-energy sound: Serum, one saw, filter on (shown
   [0:45](https://youtu.be/kSXHTJJ7D34?t=45): MG Low 24, cutoff about halfway).
2. [0:55](https://youtu.be/kSXHTJJ7D34?t=55) Chords: one note written across the loop, then bass notes under it
   (he names C, B flat, D sharp, G, F), then the top notes moved to major or minor thirds
   ([1:51](https://youtu.be/kSXHTJJ7D34?t=111)). Shown [2:20](https://youtu.be/kSXHTJJ7D34?t=140): five two-note
   chords over eight bars (2, 2, 2, 1, 1), with an on-screen note defining minor and major thirds.
3. [2:57](https://youtu.be/kSXHTJJ7D34?t=177) High-energy version: ENV 2 onto the cutoff with sustain at zero
   (shown [3:25](https://youtu.be/kSXHTJJ7D34?t=205): amount 68, later 60; decay 1.00 s), reverb, RC-20; then a
   rhythm modelled on a track he likes, dotted eighths and passing notes (shown
   [4:15](https://youtu.be/kSXHTJJ7D34?t=255)).
4. [4:44](https://youtu.be/kSXHTJJ7D34?t=284) The premise: two 16-bar blocks (shown
   [5:00](https://youtu.be/kSXHTJJ7D34?t=300): the pad clip duplicated, the pluck clip after it).
5. [5:26](https://youtu.be/kSXHTJJ7D34?t=326) Relaxed block: an airy pad from the same patch (unison, ENV 1
   release 4.79 s shown [6:00](https://youtu.be/kSXHTJJ7D34?t=360), heavy reverb), a one-bar volume swell in
   ShaperBox 3 (shown [7:00](https://youtu.be/kSXHTJJ7D34?t=420): mix 53 %), a distorted pad made off camera.
6. [7:30](https://youtu.be/kSXHTJJ7D34?t=450) Halfway: an open hat and a 16th hat loop, plus a wider layer of the
   chords ([7:50](https://youtu.be/kSXHTJJ7D34?t=470)).
7. [8:12](https://youtu.be/kSXHTJJ7D34?t=492) High-energy block: bass from the pluck part's notes (bottom notes
   only, octave found by ear, an 808-style patch from his own pack), notes shortened slightly
   ([8:54](https://youtu.be/kSXHTJJ7D34?t=534)).
8. [9:14](https://youtu.be/kSXHTJJ7D34?t=554) Drums: muffled kick, layered open hat, closed hat off the grid,
   darker clap (shown [9:30](https://youtu.be/kSXHTJJ7D34?t=570)); then the hidden percussion loop
   ([9:35](https://youtu.be/kSXHTJJ7D34?t=575)).
9. [9:57](https://youtu.be/kSXHTJJ7D34?t=597) The whole layout (shown
   [10:15](https://youtu.be/kSXHTJJ7D34?t=615)): bars 1 to 16 relaxed, hats from bar 9; bars 17 to 32 pluck
   chords, bass and drums from bar 25. Playback with his comments: a vocal verse would sit in the relaxed block
   ([10:38](https://youtu.be/kSXHTJJ7D34?t=638)); the first half of the pluck block needs nothing else
   ([11:03](https://youtu.be/kSXHTJJ7D34?t=663)).

## Tools the creator uses
- Ableton Live (arrangement, groove pool). Rohil: Logic.
- Xfer Serum (version 1): pad, pluck, airy pad; an 808-style bass preset from his "Orbital" pack.
- XLN RC-20 Retro Color on the pluck, to rough it up and widen it. Rohil: Ensemble or Chorus plus ChromaGlow, or
  STEREOVAULT; the tape wobble is lost.
- Cableguys ShaperBox 3 (Volume) for the pad swell. Rohil: Kickstart 2 at one cycle per bar, or a Serum LFO; the
  drawn ramp is lost.
- Visible in the browser or on strips, not discussed: OTT, Echo, EQ Eight, FabFilter Pro-Q 2,
  ValhallaVintageVerb.
- Samples: hat loops, an impact and a short effect, kick, clap and percussion one-shots (names partly readable).
