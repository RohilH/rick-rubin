## What it is about
He remixes "Sweet Lovin" by Me & You in Logic Pro, on camera, with a three-step order of work he calls the 3-step
flip: new chords under the vocal, a style and direction proved by building the drop, then the arrangement. The
remix turns a relaxed vocal song into what he calls stutter house at 126 BPM by making a new lead out of the
vocal itself. The arrangement part is mostly about transitions and automation. This is the closest of the four
videos to what Rohil is doing now (a house remix of a vocal track), and it is in his DAW.

## The main ideas
1. **Chords first, because they set the mood of everything.** The first decision in a remix is new harmony under
   the fixed vocal, found from the bottom notes up ([0:41](https://youtu.be/bx8X7oiYhCg?t=41),
   [1:23](https://youtu.be/bx8X7oiYhCg?t=83)).
2. **A direction is one sentence, and only a finished drop tests it.** Pick a genre or not, say what happens to
   the vocal, then build the complete drop before arranging, because only that shows whether the idea deserves a
   song ([2:45](https://youtu.be/bx8X7oiYhCg?t=165), [7:23](https://youtu.be/bx8X7oiYhCg?t=443)).
3. **The energy lives in the transitions.** Blocks laid end to end do not make a song; if the joins into the
   breakdown and the drop are dull, nobody reaches the drop ([9:50](https://youtu.be/bx8X7oiYhCg?t=590)).
4. **Everything is moving at all times.** Each part has one automated parameter, and the outgoing and incoming
   leads move in opposite directions ([10:10](https://youtu.be/bx8X7oiYhCg?t=610),
   [12:23](https://youtu.be/bx8X7oiYhCg?t=743)).
5. **A breakdown has a job:** phase the intro's lead out and the drop's lead in
   ([9:29](https://youtu.be/bx8X7oiYhCg?t=569), [11:58](https://youtu.be/bx8X7oiYhCg?t=718)).
6. **Derive, do not invent.** The lead is made from the vocal, the drop chords are the intro chords in an airier
   sound, the bass is the chords' bottom notes ([3:25](https://youtu.be/bx8X7oiYhCg?t=205),
   [4:31](https://youtu.be/bx8X7oiYhCg?t=271), [7:30](https://youtu.be/bx8X7oiYhCg?t=450)).
7. **Ask.** He got the artists' yes before starting; they may play it out
   ([0:21](https://youtu.be/bx8X7oiYhCg?t=21)).

## Step by step
1. [0:13](https://youtu.be/bx8X7oiYhCg?t=13) Step 0: pick the song; permission from the artists.
2. [0:41](https://youtu.be/bx8X7oiYhCg?t=41) Step 1: the vocal alone, then chords on a plain sound. Shown
   [1:15](https://youtu.be/bx8X7oiYhCg?t=75): Serum, one saw, MG Low 24, track named "tight pads".
3. [1:23](https://youtu.be/bx8X7oiYhCg?t=83) Bottom notes first; thirds added, fifths skipped
   ([1:43](https://youtu.be/bx8X7oiYhCg?t=103)); a pedal and passing notes
   ([2:03](https://youtu.be/bx8X7oiYhCg?t=123)). Shown [2:15](https://youtu.be/bx8X7oiYhCg?t=135): two bars per
   chord; bottom notes F#, G#, F# under bars 3 to 8 (the first is off screen), thirds A#, B, A#, a D# held on top
   throughout and a G# added in bars 7 to 8. Project key signature B major (shown
   [10:45](https://youtu.be/bx8X7oiYhCg?t=645)).
4. [2:45](https://youtu.be/bx8X7oiYhCg?t=165) Step 2: direction. His: chop the vocal and make it stutter for a
   higher-energy track.
5. [3:25](https://youtu.be/bx8X7oiYhCg?t=205) One phrase bounced and loaded into Pigments' granular engine (shown
   [3:55](https://youtu.be/bx8X7oiYhCg?t=235)); a volume shaper for the stutter, reverb, OTT at 48 % depth (shown
   [4:15](https://youtu.be/bx8X7oiYhCg?t=255)).
6. [4:31](https://youtu.be/bx8X7oiYhCg?t=271) Drop chords: the intro chords on an airier patch. He tries a sine,
   then settles on high unison with the cutoff lowered (shown [5:30](https://youtu.be/bx8X7oiYhCg?t=330): OSC A
   unison 10, OSC B saw unison 9, MG Low 24), and picks the octave by ear.
7. [6:19](https://youtu.be/bx8X7oiYhCg?t=379) Drums: a "progressive house kick", a hat made of two layers (shown
   [6:40](https://youtu.be/bx8X7oiYhCg?t=400)), a garage-style clap, a shaker loop taken from a student.
8. [7:23](https://youtu.be/bx8X7oiYhCg?t=443) Bass: the chord synth duplicated, bottom notes only, an octave down
   so the fundamental is at 40 to 80 Hz (shown [8:15](https://youtu.be/bx8X7oiYhCg?t=495): Channel EQ analyzer,
   peak near 45 Hz), sub oscillator sine added. He listens to the unprocessed idea and decides to go on.
9. [9:09](https://youtu.be/bx8X7oiYhCg?t=549) Step 3: rough blocks. Intro 16 bars, breakdown bars 17 to 24, drop
   at bar 25 (shown [9:35](https://youtu.be/bx8X7oiYhCg?t=575)).
10. [10:30](https://youtu.be/bx8X7oiYhCg?t=630) Vocal reverb automation: ValhallaShimmer wet/dry 0.12 rising to
    about 0.55 by the breakdown, back to 0.11 at bar 25 (shown [10:45](https://youtu.be/bx8X7oiYhCg?t=645)).
11. [10:55](https://youtu.be/bx8X7oiYhCg?t=655) Chords: Serum cutoff from about 200 Hz to 1087 Hz toward bar 17
    (shown [11:12](https://youtu.be/bx8X7oiYhCg?t=672)).
12. [11:38](https://youtu.be/bx8X7oiYhCg?t=698) The drop's shaker borrowed into the intro.
13. [11:58](https://youtu.be/bx8X7oiYhCg?t=718) Breakdown: the stutter lead filtered in, its reverb mix falling
    0.53 to 0.08 (shown [12:35](https://youtu.be/bx8X7oiYhCg?t=755)), the mirror of the vocal.
14. [12:43](https://youtu.be/bx8X7oiYhCg?t=763) Transition effects, then off-camera fine-tuning; he mentions
    adding a "BP thing" to the intro.
15. [13:25](https://youtu.be/bx8X7oiYhCg?t=805) Playback. Finished project shown
    [13:32](https://youtu.be/bx8X7oiYhCg?t=812): the bar map is in the system card. The original vocal returns
    low-passed, "underwater" ([14:34](https://youtu.be/bx8X7oiYhCg?t=874)).

## Tools the creator uses
- Logic Pro (Channel EQ and its analyzer, track automation, bounce in place).
- Xfer Serum (version 1, he says) for chords, drop pads and bass: saw, MG Low 24, sub oscillator.
- Arturia Pigments: granular engine, to turn a vocal phrase into a held, playable note. Rohil: Serum 2's granular
  oscillator or Logic's Alchemy.
- A volume shaper for the stutter (ShaperBox partly visible on the strip). Rohil: Logic Tremolo, or MIDI.
- Xfer OTT on the stutter lead (Rohil owns it). Valhalla VintageVerb on the lead, ValhallaShimmer on the vocal
  (Rohil: Supermassive or ChromaVerb; no octave shimmer).
- Seen on strips, not discussed: Ozone 11, Saturn 2, Kickstart, Exciter, Chorus, St-Delay, a "DRUM VERB" bus.
- Samples: PML "Boehmer" kick, clap and hats; "Ebb and Flow Beat" shaker; MARS 808 tom; KSHMR effects.
