## What it is about
Ryos builds a bass house drop from an empty FL Studio project at 126 BPM, as an idea for a VIP mix of one of
his releases. Only the drop is made, plus a quick filler build at the end to hear it in context. The drop is a
call and response between a nasty long-note lead and a stack of short stabs, over a sub and simple drums, and
then about half the session goes into ear candy and variation. He talks while working and gives almost no
numbers; names and chains below come from the screen. The key appears to be C sharp minor (inferred from a
sample label and the notes read off the piano roll).

## The main ideas
1. **Simple melody, wild production.** In bass house the notes should be simple and the effort goes into
   sound, production tricks and arrangement. [1:23](https://youtu.be/N_RgytoSM94?t=83)
2. **Sounds first.** He hunts for "disgusting" lead presets and bases the drop on one, before any notes.
   [0:41](https://youtu.be/N_RgytoSM94?t=41), [1:03](https://youtu.be/N_RgytoSM94?t=63)
3. **Call and response**, each side its own group of layers. [2:04](https://youtu.be/N_RgytoSM94?t=124)
4. **Get a simple drum loop in early**; it tells you how to proceed with the basses.
   [3:10](https://youtu.be/N_RgytoSM94?t=190)
5. **Layer until the missing quality appears**: a crisp top, then a plucky bass for bounce.
   [4:33](https://youtu.be/N_RgytoSM94?t=273), [5:14](https://youtu.be/N_RgytoSM94?t=314)
6. **The core first, then the fun part**: vocal cuts, percussion, stabs, synth cuts. This is where it starts
   to sound good. [6:46](https://youtu.be/N_RgytoSM94?t=406)
7. **Try, listen, keep or cut**, including accidents: an LFO idea is removed, an accidental unmute is kept, a
   low-mid layer is added on trial. [6:24](https://youtu.be/N_RgytoSM94?t=384), [9:30](https://youtu.be/N_RgytoSM94?t=570)
8. **Vary the second time around** and give the two halves different drums.
   [11:27](https://youtu.be/N_RgytoSM94?t=687), [14:24](https://youtu.be/N_RgytoSM94?t=864)
9. **Hear the drop in context** before judging it: a quick build. [14:44](https://youtu.be/N_RgytoSM94?t=884)

## Step by step
- [1:03](https://youtu.be/N_RgytoSM94?t=63) Call lead: a Serum preset (name reads roughly "LEAD - Harmoniq 2")
  with two complex wavetables; he changes the LFO timing and opens it up (shown 1:24).
- [2:00](https://youtu.be/N_RgytoSM94?t=120) Call notes: three long notes over two bars (shown). Response: short
  syncopated notes in the next two bars on a second Serum lead (shown 2:16).
- [2:24](https://youtu.be/N_RgytoSM94?t=144) Response lead chain: Pro-Q 3 (sub cut), CamelCrusher, OTT, then
  more EQ incl. stereo clean-up (shown 2:41, 4:17). A support layer, Serum "LD Hooked" (shown 2:51).
- [3:10](https://youtu.be/N_RgytoSM94?t=190) Sub line; a knocking kick, clap and a hat loop ("Ghost Hat Loop"
  from his October 2023 pack, shown 4:03).
- [3:51](https://youtu.be/N_RgytoSM94?t=231) Response group with Kickstart 2 at 1/4, mix 100 % (shown 4:03); the
  sub is sidechained too.
- [4:12](https://youtu.be/N_RgytoSM94?t=252) Heavy EQ on the harsh response lead (a wide dip near 3.85 kHz,
  shown 4:17). A crisp top layer; group chain Pultec-style EQ, soothe2, Supercharger, Kickstart 2 (shown 4:51).
- [5:14](https://youtu.be/N_RgytoSM94?t=314) A plucky bass layer adds the bounce.
- [5:41](https://youtu.be/N_RgytoSM94?t=341) Fake-out: Kickstart mix automated off for the first two bars
  (shown 5:58); Endless Smile intensity ramped on the call lead (shown 6:20); LFOTool tried and dropped.
- [6:24](https://youtu.be/N_RgytoSM94?t=384) White noise layered with the call lead.
- [7:06](https://youtu.be/N_RgytoSM94?t=426) Ear candy: a clap figure, a filler over the kick, a ride held in
  reserve, a looped atmosphere slice lowered and sidechained (shown 8:37), a background synth, stabs.
- [9:30](https://youtu.be/N_RgytoSM94?t=570) Hype vocal from Splice, sped up. Rattle ride layered with the ride
  ([10:13](https://youtu.be/N_RgytoSM94?t=613)). Bass stabs, reverse synths, a bass shot
  ([10:37](https://youtu.be/N_RgytoSM94?t=637)).
- [11:27](https://youtu.be/N_RgytoSM94?t=687) Second pass: LFO wobble on the call via LFOTool with rate and mix
  automation (shown 11:42), sub added, a faster spot.
- [11:48](https://youtu.be/N_RgytoSM94?t=708) Fill: Endless Smile as reverb on the vocal chop, a drum fill.
  The bass shot follows the sub's two notes the second time ([12:29](https://youtu.be/N_RgytoSM94?t=749)).
- [12:49](https://youtu.be/N_RgytoSM94?t=769) A meaty supporting bass for low mids under the call, top cut off
  and compressed ([13:17](https://youtu.be/N_RgytoSM94?t=797)). The fill is replaced; the bass shot turned up.
- [14:03](https://youtu.be/N_RgytoSM94?t=843) Tambourine; an offbeat ride kept only for the response half; a
  hip-hop siren ([14:44](https://youtu.be/N_RgytoSM94?t=884)). Quick build, playback at
  [15:05](https://youtu.be/N_RgytoSM94?t=905).
- Final track list (shown 11:42): Filler Clap, Rattle Ride, Ride, Call LD, Accents, Response LD, a vocal
  texture, an atmos, a pulse, Stab, Bass Shot, Sub, Alt Sub, Vocal, Noise Shots.

## Tools the creator uses
- **DAW:** FL Studio 21. Master: iZotope Insight (metering), Sonarworks Reference 4 (monitor correction).
- **Synth:** Xfer Serum for every lead, layer, bass and sub.
- **Effects:** FabFilter Pro-Q 3 (cuts, harshness, stereo clean-up), CamelCrusher (distortion), Xfer OTT,
  UAD Pultec EQP-1A, oeksound soothe2, NI Supercharger (group compression), Kickstart 2 (all sidechain, mix
  automated), Dada Life Endless Smile (fake-out wash, fill reverb), Xfer LFOTool (wobble), Fruity Balance
  (volume automation), a tuner on the response lead.
- **Samples:** his monthly Patreon packs (hat loop, atmos shot, fills, white noise), Splice ("hype vocal").
