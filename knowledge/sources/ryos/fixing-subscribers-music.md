## What it is about
Ryos loads the stems of a subscriber's vocal progressive house track (128 BPM) into FL Studio and reworks it
in about twelve minutes of edited footage. His verdict: the break is impressive, the drop falls short. He
rebuilds the drop as a bouncier "hybrid prog" drop on the same melody, then tightens the break, a big
half-time "hands up" section and the build. Most of the work is fast and from his own presets and samples, so
few numbers are spoken; chains and names below were read off the screen. The value for Rohil is watching what
an experienced producer keeps, what he mutes and what he replaces in someone else's nearly finished track.

## The main ideas
1. **Keep what works, replace the concept that does not.** The kick, the fills, the vocal and the intro chords
   stay; the drop's style changes. [1:15](https://youtu.be/23NY1XxOlbA?t=75), [4:33](https://youtu.be/23NY1XxOlbA?t=273)
2. **Bounce comes from note length and rhythm, not new notes**: shorter lead notes, a bass in the lead's
   rhythm with octave jumps, chords changing every bar. [2:09](https://youtu.be/23NY1XxOlbA?t=129),
   [3:53](https://youtu.be/23NY1XxOlbA?t=233)
3. **A break improves by subtraction.** Mute the soft pluck, the early bass and the bright ambience; open with
   the vocal and one or two synths; return parts in stages. [7:47](https://youtu.be/23NY1XxOlbA?t=467)
4. **A good vocal belongs in the drop**: one phrase, washed, printed and shaped into a texture.
   [6:22](https://youtu.be/23NY1XxOlbA?t=382)
5. **Low end is one thing at a time.** Kick, sub boom and bass together is a clash: delete one, sidechain the
   other. [10:15](https://youtu.be/23NY1XxOlbA?t=615)
6. **Ear candy fills the vocal's gaps**, and the same trick is not used twice in a row.
   [8:29](https://youtu.be/23NY1XxOlbA?t=509), [9:12](https://youtu.be/23NY1XxOlbA?t=552)
7. **Reuse saved chains and stacks** from earlier projects to move fast. [2:51](https://youtu.be/23NY1XxOlbA?t=171),
   [10:57](https://youtu.be/23NY1XxOlbA?t=657)

## Step by step
- [0:41](https://youtu.be/23NY1XxOlbA?t=41) Plays the original. [1:15](https://youtu.be/23NY1XxOlbA?t=75)
  Diagnosis and plan.
- [1:35](https://youtu.be/23NY1XxOlbA?t=95) Lead stack on the subscriber's melody, notes shortened and a few
  pitches refined (shown 2:02). Layers: a Spire lead; a buzzy, gritty Spire layer, preset "AS_LD - Digital
  Punk" (shown 2:32); a warm layer with a saved chain.
- [2:51](https://youtu.be/23NY1XxOlbA?t=171) Pitch automation on the leads: a clip on Spire's bend wheel with a
  quick dive at the phrase end (shown 3:10).
- [3:11](https://youtu.be/23NY1XxOlbA?t=191) Bass: the lead's rhythm on the roots G and D sharp (shown 3:35),
  Serum patch "RYOS_LD - Could Be A Bass" (shown 3:52), octave jumps, a sub, then FocusOne and Kickstart 2 on
  the bass group (shown 4:24). Tracks are named NRG Bass, Bouncy Bass, Sub. Roots now change every bar.
- [4:33](https://youtu.be/23NY1XxOlbA?t=273) Drums: his kick kept and turned up; his fills reused; a clap and
  snare added. Fills with a ride printed over them are faded in or replaced by Ryos's own laser and clap fill.
  A noise ride and crash for the top end ([6:02](https://youtu.be/23NY1XxOlbA?t=362)).
- [6:22](https://youtu.be/23NY1XxOlbA?t=382) Vocal shot for the drop: Supermassive wash, recorded to audio,
  Pro-Q 3 "Phone" preset with the highs reopened (shown 6:58), PanMan, sidechain, OTT at 100 % (shown 7:27),
  plus the lead group's delay. Lead group chain on screen at 7:27: ValhallaDelay, ValhallaRoom, Supercharger,
  soothe2, a Pultec-style EQ, Pro-Q 3, Kickstart 2.
- [7:47](https://youtu.be/23NY1XxOlbA?t=467) Break: soft pluck muted, no bass until the second half of the
  verse (a reese), bright ambient stem muted until the reese and drums enter.
- [8:29](https://youtu.be/23NY1XxOlbA?t=509) Delay throw on the word "lost": H-Delay at 1/4 (shown 9:00), high
  pass up, ping-pong off, an ensemble and a reverb after it. A synth answers the next key word instead.
- [9:35](https://youtu.be/23NY1XxOlbA?t=575) Snaps added to the second break; cinematic drums EQ'd less boomy; a
  sweep into the chord section.
- [9:55](https://youtu.be/23NY1XxOlbA?t=595) "Hands up" section: hardstyle-type kicks moved to hit with the
  chords; the bass removed where the sub boom lands, the boom sidechained; his own larger chord stack; LFOTool
  tremolo with rising amount (shown 11:34; chord group chain: Waves C4, Pultec-style EQ, Pro-Q 3, Kickstart 2,
  LFOTool); a cleaner bass.
- [12:04](https://youtu.be/23NY1XxOlbA?t=724) Build: drop leads copied in, with Simplon, Endless Smile (preset
  reads "smack in the face") and Fruity Balance automated (shown 12:18); the vocal phrase placed right before
  the drop; one more riser.
- [13:05](https://youtu.be/23NY1XxOlbA?t=785) Plays the result.

## Tools the creator uses
- **DAW:** FL Studio 21. **Synths:** Reveal Sound Spire (lead layers), Serum (bass, sub).
- **Effects:** Valhalla Supermassive (vocal wash), FabFilter Pro-Q 3 (telephone EQ and general EQ), Soundtoys
  PanMan (auto-pan), Xfer OTT (vocal shot), Kickstart 2 (sidechain on lead, bass and chord groups, mix
  automated), FocusOne (bass compression and drive), ValhallaDelay and ValhallaRoom (lead space), NI
  Supercharger (lead compression), oeksound soothe2 (harshness), UAD Pultec EQP-1A (group EQ), Waves C4 (chord
  group multiband), Waves H-Delay (vocal throw), an ensemble/chorus and a reverb on the throw, Xfer LFOTool
  (tremolo), FabFilter Simplon, Dada Life Endless Smile and Fruity Balance (build automation).
- **Samples:** his "Knight", "LVL" and Patreon packs (snares, snaps, fills, laser, noise ride, risers).
