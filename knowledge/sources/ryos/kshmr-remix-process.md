## What it is about
Despite the title this is not a start-to-finish remix walkthrough. It is one free chapter of his paid course
on his remix of KSHMR's "Wild Ride": the synths of the two breaks, taken track by track in the finished FL
Studio project (130 BPM, shown). The chapters on the break bass (before) and the vocal (after), the drop, the
mix and the master are not in the video. What it does show about remixing: the original vocal and one new
bass are the stars, every other break sound is chosen to stay out of their way, pieces of the acapella and
stems from his own old tracks are recycled, and the arrangement follows the vocal's structure. Most plug-in
settings below were read off the screen.

## The main ideas
1. **Decide the main characters, then only support them.** In the break the bass is the "main character"
   and the vocal is the only bright element, so every added sound is an atmosphere: dark, spacious, and
   technically out of the way. [3:57](https://youtu.be/mvZPg8zv_ig?t=237)
2. **Something interesting every eight bars**, and the additions are ordered: "slowly pack on the
   frequencies", from thin atmospheres to an arp with bite to chords and pads.
   [8:05](https://youtu.be/mvZPg8zv_ig?t=485), [10:53](https://youtu.be/mvZPg8zv_ig?t=653),
   [21:41](https://youtu.be/mvZPg8zv_ig?t=1301)
3. **Follow the vocal.** Chords enter for the first time where the vocal repeats and its harmonies come in.
   [19:50](https://youtu.be/mvZPg8zv_ig?t=1190)
4. **Recycle.** A stutter in KSHMR's acapella becomes an atmospheric throw; stems from his own released and
   unreleased tracks are dragged in and pitched to the key. [4:38](https://youtu.be/mvZPg8zv_ig?t=278),
   [3:36](https://youtu.be/mvZPg8zv_ig?t=216), [8:26](https://youtu.be/mvZPg8zv_ig?t=506)
5. **A supporting sound has one band.** The arp, the horn and the "crispy" bass are thinned until they live
   in the high mids and highs: what would be masked anyway only eats headroom, and low mids make a sound
   feel close, which is for vocals and drop leads. [16:18](https://youtu.be/mvZPg8zv_ig?t=978),
   [24:52](https://youtu.be/mvZPg8zv_ig?t=1492)
6. **Width is managed, not maximised.** Wide samples are pulled back until the correlation meter stays at
   or above zero; lows narrower than mids, mids narrower than highs.
   [9:07](https://youtu.be/mvZPg8zv_ig?t=547), [17:44](https://youtu.be/mvZPg8zv_ig?t=1064)
7. **Atmosphere means delay plus reverb**, and a bright washed reverb can supply the top end an arrangement
   lacks. [6:23](https://youtu.be/mvZPg8zv_ig?t=383), [3:11](https://youtu.be/mvZPg8zv_ig?t=191)
8. **Filtering is dull and "critical"**: every track ends in the same utility plug-ins so filter, level and
   pump can be automated. [1:24](https://youtu.be/mvZPg8zv_ig?t=84), [19:29](https://youtu.be/mvZPg8zv_ig?t=1169)

## Step by step
- [0:41](https://youtu.be/mvZPg8zv_ig?t=41) "Buzzer Flutter Bed": a gated vocal atmosphere from his pack, up 2
  semitones to the key (said). Chain: Endless Smile, Simplon, Fruity Balance, a gain plug-in, Kickstart 2
  (shown 1:40). It starts low-passed and opens as the break goes on ([7:03](https://youtu.be/mvZPg8zv_ig?t=423)).
- [2:06](https://youtu.be/mvZPg8zv_ig?t=126) Endless Smile used as a reverb, left at intensity 50 %, preset
  "Fist in the Air" (shown 2:30): it thins the signal and adds a bright wash, to create highs the break lacks.
- [3:36](https://youtu.be/mvZPg8zv_ig?t=216) "TOU Slideys": horn-like slides taken as a stem from an old song
  of his, up 2 semitones; placed a few times so the break keeps surprising.
- [4:38](https://youtu.be/mvZPg8zv_ig?t=278) "LFO Vocal Candy": a stutter sliced out of the acapella. Chain
  (shown 5:25): Noveltech Character, DST with high mids and highs raised, OTT (depth 33 %, time 100 %, shown
  6:08), ValhallaDelay (Tape, single, 1/8 dotted, mix 23.6 %, feedback 80 %, age 60 %, wow 60 %, flutter 50 %,
  preset "Instant Vibe"), Supermassive (Hydra, mix 32 %, delay 1/4, warp 67.3 %, feedback 62.7 %, density
  80.3 %, mod 0.94 Hz at 63.7 %, low cut 140 Hz, his preset "Ryos Airy Verb"; both shown 6:20). Three short
  clips, late in bars 10 (intro), 22 and 30 (shown 5:00).
- [8:26](https://youtu.be/mvZPg8zv_ig?t=506) "Vocal Atmos Bed": a vocal bed from an unreleased song, up 1
  semitone. Only insert: MSED with side gain -5.3 dB (shown 9:50), because the sample was out of phase.
- [10:53](https://youtu.be/mvZPg8zv_ig?t=653) "Sick Arp": one held note of an Output Arcade sound turned into
  an arp. Chain (shown 12:05): ShaperBox 3 volume (four decaying spikes per beat, mix 79 %), RC-20 ("Lush &
  Crunch Guitar 1", shown 12:45), Thermal ("Vapor Waiver", said), DST (lows and low mids down, high mids and
  highs up), ValhallaUberMod (stereo flange, said), OTT, Kickstart 2 (light, constant), Pro-Q 4 tilt shelf at
  900 Hz, +2.4 dB (shown 15:45), Pro-Q 4 low cut at 344 Hz, 24 dB/oct (shown 16:15), SpaceControl with phase
  limits 28 / 34.7 / 50 for the bands above 1 kHz, 200 Hz to 1 kHz and below 200 Hz (shown 17:30). A second
  mixer track adds an EQ automated thinner for the intro, plus filter and volume automation (18:26 to 19:08).
- [19:29](https://youtu.be/mvZPg8zv_ig?t=1169) "Classic Poly Pad": a plain saw pad, the first chords of the
  break, with sidechain and SpaceControl. With it a washed supersaw pad ("ASOT Pad"), Character only
  ([20:56](https://youtu.be/mvZPg8zv_ig?t=1256)).
- [22:02](https://youtu.be/mvZPg8zv_ig?t=1322) Second half of the song, "hands up" section, new sounds: a Nexus
  dance piano with only RVox (comp -11.0, gain -3.7, shown 22:50); an Analog Lab horn he calls "Analog Drone"
  with heavy distortion, Supermassive and a thinning EQ ([23:02](https://youtu.be/mvZPg8zv_ig?t=1382)); a
  "Wind Up Siren" made from a pack sample with pitch automation, a fade and added highs
  ([25:35](https://youtu.be/mvZPg8zv_ig?t=1535)); a "Crispy Bass" (a Serum 2 preset by Dyro, shown 27:35) with
  only OTT, squeezed hard, highs boosted ([26:18](https://youtu.be/mvZPg8zv_ig?t=1578)).
- Section markers on his timeline (shown [16:15](https://youtu.be/mvZPg8zv_ig?t=975), read at 720p, give or
  take a bar): Intro 1, Break 17, Build 35, Drop 43, 2nd Break 59, Hook 75, Hands Up 83, Build 91, Drop 99.

## Tools the creator uses
- **DAW:** FL Studio; mixer buses named Refs, MASTER, MIX BUS, INSTRUMENTAL, ACAPELLA (shown 1:40).
- **Sources:** his Patreon pack samples, stems from his own songs, Output Arcade (arp source), reFX Nexus
  (dance piano), Arturia Analog Lab (horn), Serum 2 (crispy bass).
- **Automation utilities on every track:** FabFilter Simplon (filters), Fruity Balance (volume), a gain
  plug-in (big gain moves), Kickstart 2 (pump).
- **Colour:** Noveltech Character (exciter), DST by Matt Nash (multiband distortion used like an EQ), OTT,
  XLN RC-20, Output Thermal (bit-crush preset), Waves RVox (two-control compressor on synths).
- **Space and motion:** Dada Life Endless Smile (as a reverb), ValhallaDelay, Valhalla Supermassive,
  ValhallaUberMod (flange), Cableguys ShaperBox 3 (volume shape), Soundtoys PanMan (present, off).
- **Width and metering:** Voxengo MSED (side gain; he credits Hardwell for the tip), Acustica SpaceControl
  (three-band width and phase limiting, one of his three most used), iZotope Insight (correlation). EQ: Pro-Q 4.
