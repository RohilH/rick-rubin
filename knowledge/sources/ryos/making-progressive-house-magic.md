## What it is about
Ryos opens FL Studio at 128 BPM with two things ready: a lead stack saved from an earlier remix and a folder of
melodies he wrote on other days. He picks one fast melody in A minor and builds a break, an 8-bar build and a
16-bar drop around it in half an hour of edited footage. The lesson is less the sounds (nearly all presets and
pack samples) than the decisions: what to slow down because the melody is fast, what has to change when break
sounds are copied into a drop, and how he fixes the kick against the sub. He names almost no numbers, so the
settings below were read off the screen. Pitches are in Logic's naming (C3 = MIDI 60); FL shows them two
octaves higher.

## The main ideas
1. **One fast part, everything else slower.** The melody runs in eighth notes, so the chords are simplified, a
   galloping bass is shelved for a held one, and the dance piano is deleted: no "counteracting rhythms".
   [1:25](https://youtu.be/srkGlUWW3pg?t=85), [3:37](https://youtu.be/srkGlUWW3pg?t=217), [18:43](https://youtu.be/srkGlUWW3pg?t=1123)
2. **A drop has different needs from a break.** The break is copied into the drop with a kick and sidechain,
   sounds "too soft", and is hardened: brighter drums, a buzzy chord layer, distorted basses, no piano.
   [12:35](https://youtu.be/srkGlUWW3pg?t=755), [14:00](https://youtu.be/srkGlUWW3pg?t=840), [16:51](https://youtu.be/srkGlUWW3pg?t=1011)
3. **Start from a bank, not a blank page.** Melodies are written on other days and saved; the lead stack comes
   from the last project; the melody is on it within two minutes. [0:42](https://youtu.be/srkGlUWW3pg?t=42)
4. **Doubt about a melody is settled by leaving it.** Unsure at the checkpoint, he notes that the tune stuck in
   his head during a break, then changes only its first notes. [19:03](https://youtu.be/srkGlUWW3pg?t=1143)
5. **Shape the sub's duck to this kick.** The sub's volume curve is drawn as the mirror of the kick sample's
   own envelope instead of a stock sidechain shape. [19:24](https://youtu.be/srkGlUWW3pg?t=1164)
6. **Supporting chords are a stack too**: strings, a horn-like patch and a clean buzzy layer for the top, low
   mids cut where pad and bass pile up. [4:18](https://youtu.be/srkGlUWW3pg?t=258), [11:35](https://youtu.be/srkGlUWW3pg?t=695)
7. **One characterful sound ties break and drop together**: a bright, dirty pluck doubles the melody in the
   break and stays in the drop. [21:23](https://youtu.be/srkGlUWW3pg?t=1283), [24:02](https://youtu.be/srkGlUWW3pg?t=1442)
8. **The build previews the drop behind filters**, bass included, sidechain left on for groove.
   [24:55](https://youtu.be/srkGlUWW3pg?t=1495)

## Step by step
- [0:20](https://youtu.be/srkGlUWW3pg?t=20) Ready sounds: the saved lead group "Memories x Sept Leads" (Avenger,
  Spire, "Main LD", "Low LD"; shown 5:50) and a piano in Analog Lab V.
- [1:04](https://youtu.be/srkGlUWW3pg?t=64) Melody from the bank, 4 bars, one rhythm cell per bar (a dotted
  quarter, then five eighths), each bar a step-wise wobble that climbs: F4 E4 F4 E4 F4 G4 / A4 G4 A4 G4 A4 B4 /
  C5 B4 C5 B4 C5 D5 / B4 E4 G4 E4 G4 A4. Chords under it: Dm then Em in bar 1, F, Am then C, G (shown 1:15).
  He simplifies the chords to anchor it (said, [1:25](https://youtu.be/srkGlUWW3pg?t=85)) and plays it on the leads (1:45).
- [2:08](https://youtu.be/srkGlUWW3pg?t=128) Bass in Serum. First a galloping rhythm with pitch-bend automation
  ([3:10](https://youtu.be/srkGlUWW3pg?t=190)); then a version with one held note per bar and a tape-stop style
  dive on the last bar ([3:37](https://youtu.be/srkGlUWW3pg?t=217)). The simple one is kept (shown 4:10).
- [4:18](https://youtu.be/srkGlUWW3pg?t=258) Strings: Spire "PD Virus Strings" with a faster attack (shown
  5:00, said). He reworks the chords on it, choosing each top note (5:01 to 5:44), then cuts the low mids of
  pad and bass, where both are heavy (said, [5:44](https://youtu.be/srkGlUWW3pg?t=344); EQ shown 6:12).
- [6:49](https://youtu.be/srkGlUWW3pg?t=409) Atmospheres in A minor from his packs: a one-off "atmos shot"
  ("Asylum Atmos (A)") and a continuous "atmos bed" ("Beauty Atmos Bed (Bmin)", pitched down). Analog Lab V
  "Ambi Horns" joins the strings (shown 8:40) and the bed is muted again ([8:46](https://youtu.be/srkGlUWW3pg?t=526)).
- [9:06](https://youtu.be/srkGlUWW3pg?t=546) Second bass: his "Eclipse" bass in Sylenth1 as the fuzzy layer, an
  octave down, lows rolled off for the sub, CamelCrusher "British Clean" as light compression (shown 10:45).
  The Serum bass becomes the mid layer: Pro-Q 3, then Decapitator with the tone turned brighter (shown 10:18).
  Sub: a pack preset on the same pitch automation as the others (said, [10:53](https://youtu.be/srkGlUWW3pg?t=653)).
- [11:35](https://youtu.be/srkGlUWW3pg?t=695) Top end for the chord group: a Serum chord preset he reads out
  as "Progressive" ("Fuzzy Chords" in the playlist), low cut near 155 Hz at 12 dB/oct (shown 12:28).
- [12:35](https://youtu.be/srkGlUWW3pg?t=755) Copies everything to a drop. Kick first, sidechain on everything
  ([13:15](https://youtu.be/srkGlUWW3pg?t=795)); verdict: too soft for a drop (14:00).
- [14:21](https://youtu.be/srkGlUWW3pg?t=861) Drums: two fills, a short tight ride, a wide clap with its
  transient shaved in LFOTool ([15:46](https://youtu.be/srkGlUWW3pg?t=946)), a second clap with a tail, a
  metallic shaker loop, then Fresh Air for sparkle ([16:31](https://youtu.be/srkGlUWW3pg?t=991)).
- [16:51](https://youtu.be/srkGlUWW3pg?t=1011) Basses for the drop: the mid bass compressed and distorted hard
  with a metallic, tinny effect mixed in; the Eclipse bass gets Decapitator after CamelCrusher for a growl
  (shown 17:42). A third, heavily distorted high-mid bass is held in reserve (said).
- [17:58](https://youtu.be/srkGlUWW3pg?t=1078) Chords for the drop: piano out, something added on the chord
  group that he names only as "OT" (18:43; OTT is our reading), then the dance piano deleted.
- [19:24](https://youtu.be/srkGlUWW3pg?t=1164) Kick against sub: LFOTool on the sub, curve drawn from the kick
  (shown 20:25; chain LFOTool, Pro-Q 3, LFOTool).
- [20:37](https://youtu.be/srkGlUWW3pg?t=1237) New first notes for the melody. Back in the break
  ([21:23](https://youtu.be/srkGlUWW3pg?t=1283)), a Serum pluck on the melody: Cymatics Origin (preset "Bitcrush",
  shown 22:15), ValhallaVintageVerb, ValhallaDelay (shown 23:00); the synth's own reverb and delay off, release
  tighter (said). RVox on the piano ([23:21](https://youtu.be/srkGlUWW3pg?t=1401)); one more chord sound an
  octave down ("Low Mid CH"); pluck tried in the drop and kept ([24:02](https://youtu.be/srkGlUWW3pg?t=1442)).
- [24:32](https://youtu.be/srkGlUWW3pg?t=1472) Build: strings and horns enter through a high-pass and low-pass
  on the chord group; both basses enter behind a Simplon filter with the sidechain kept; the leads open with a
  filter; one fragment of the melody is looped ([25:39](https://youtu.be/srkGlUWW3pg?t=1539)).
- [26:00](https://youtu.be/srkGlUWW3pg?t=1560) Build drums: snare, kick, clap. FX: a downlifter, a laser riser,
  a "dirty pong" riser, a standard riser (27:16); the snare's pitch automated upward (29:27).
- [29:49](https://youtu.be/srkGlUWW3pg?t=1789) Details: the spray fill reused, crash layers on the downbeat, a
  clap as a placeholder for the drop's second half. Final layout (shown 31:20): break bars 17 to 25 (pluck,
  low-mid chords, piano), build 25 to 33 with a reversed lead before the drop, drop from 33 (16 bars earlier
  at 25:05; nothing is seen in the bars opened in front).

## Tools the creator uses
- **DAW:** FL Studio. **Synths:** Vengeance Avenger and Reveal Sound Spire (lead stack, strings), Serum (mid
  bass, chord layer, pluck, sub), Sylenth1 (Eclipse bass), Arturia Analog Lab V (piano, "Ambi Horns").
- **Effects:** FabFilter Pro-Q 3 and Simplon (EQ, filter sweeps), Soundtoys Decapitator and CamelCrusher (bass
  drive), Xfer LFOTool (clap transient, sub duck), Kickstart 2 (pump), Slate Fresh Air (drum sparkle), Cymatics
  Origin (lo-fi colour on the pluck), ValhallaVintageVerb and ValhallaDelay, Waves RVox (quick compression),
  Dada Life Endless Smile and Fruity Balance (build automation, shown 29:45).
- **Samples:** his October 2024 Patreon pack and "Ultimate Collection" (kick, fills, rides, claps, shaker
  loops, risers, atmospheres, bass presets), a wide clap one-shot found by searching "wide clap".
