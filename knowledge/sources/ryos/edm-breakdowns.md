## What it is about
An excerpt from Ryos's paid course about his track "I Told You" (128 BPM, FL Studio). He opens the finished
project at the breakdown and explains its two main sounds: a vocal chop and a dark chord part he calls the
"vibey chords". For each he shows where the sound came from, the notes, and every plug-in in the chain, and
says why. The video stops before the supporting sounds (backing synth, arp, strings, reese) are discussed. The
track is a fast, dark, big-room-leaning one, but the reasoning carries over to any break.

## The main ideas
1. **Two strong sounds must carry the feeling before anything is layered.** Adding sound after sound in
   search of the "magic" only makes clutter. [14:30](https://youtu.be/l_XjHmumqQk?t=870)
2. **One progression for break and drop** is the safe way to make a track cohesive; the cost is repetition,
   paid for with constant change in the arrangement. A vocal song may switch chords in the pre-chorus, and the
   hook's chords are then the drop's. [8:32](https://youtu.be/l_XjHmumqQk?t=512)
3. **Less harmony is darker.** Triads without their third, then inversions chosen so the top notes make the
   line he wants. He says to always check what the top of a chord progression is playing.
   [10:17](https://youtu.be/l_XjHmumqQk?t=617), [11:00](https://youtu.be/l_XjHmumqQk?t=660)
4. **Sound-design decisions and mixing decisions are different things**, even when both are an EQ: cutting a
   bell's whole top end is design; trimming 145 Hz is mixing. [12:21](https://youtu.be/l_XjHmumqQk?t=741)
5. **Brighten with distortion, not only EQ**; a multiband distortion adds harmonics a dull sample lacks.
   [3:31](https://youtu.be/l_XjHmumqQk?t=211)
6. **Extreme delay and reverb on the insert "transport" the listener**: he wants a production to feel like
   an arena and a journey. [5:47](https://youtu.be/l_XjHmumqQk?t=347)
7. **Dynamic EQ for harsh frequencies**, because a deep static notch can sound nasal.
   [6:29](https://youtu.be/l_XjHmumqQk?t=389)
8. **Work in two stages**: a production project, then stems into a separate mix and master project.
   [7:30](https://youtu.be/l_XjHmumqQk?t=450)

## Step by step
- [1:03](https://youtu.be/l_XjHmumqQk?t=63) Plays the break. Track list shown at 1:40: Vocal Chop, Vibey
  Chords, Backing Synth, TOU Accent Synth, Arp, Tension String, Reese, Dirty Bass, Leads, Chant layer. Playlist
  markers read Intro, Big Synths, Drop, Drop Out, Vocal, Break Kick, Big Synth.
- [2:05](https://youtu.be/l_XjHmumqQk?t=125) Vocal chop: Output Arcade, one key (G) held, cut at each bar so
  the phrase restarts, one small gap cut (said; one long note shown 2:30). Opened in an older project save
  because the instrument was later deleted.
- [3:09](https://youtu.be/l_XjHmumqQk?t=189) Chop chain, in order (shown 3:45): DST, ValhallaDelay,
  ValhallaSupermassive, FabFilter Pro-Q 3. DST's band knobs are raised like an EQ (said).
- [4:52](https://youtu.be/l_XjHmumqQk?t=292) ValhallaDelay (shown): Tape mode, Dual style, 1/4 and 1/4, mix
  20.8 %, feedback 87.6 %, width 100 %, drive 18 dB, age 100 %, wow 63 %, flutter 66 %, low cut 530 Hz.
- [5:18](https://youtu.be/l_XjHmumqQk?t=318) Supermassive (shown): Sagittarius, "Best Ballad Vocal", mix 24.4 %,
  delay 245.7 ms, warp 22.4 %, feedback 86 %, density 10 %, mod 0.32 Hz / 78.8 %, EQ 4080 Hz and 400 Hz.
- [6:07](https://youtu.be/l_XjHmumqQk?t=367) Pro-Q 3: steep low cut plus a narrow dynamic band at about
  1.78 kHz (shown 6:18); it dips hard on the first hit and rests at about 3 to 6 dB of cut (said).
- [8:11](https://youtu.be/l_XjHmumqQk?t=491) Vibey chords MIDI (shown 8:25): G for two bars, D sharp and C for
  one bar each; voicings G G D, D sharp A sharp D sharp, C G C.
- [9:56](https://youtu.be/l_XjHmumqQk?t=596) How he got there: triads, minus the thirds, then the upper notes
  of chords 2 and 3 taken down an octave so the top line reads D, D sharp, C.
- [12:01](https://youtu.be/l_XjHmumqQk?t=721) Sound: Analog Lab V "Bouncy Bells" (shown 12:13). Chain (shown):
  Pro-Q 3 high cut near 1 kHz; FocusOne (focus on extreme, some drive and width); Pro-Q 3 with a low cut near
  145 Hz and a 36 dB/oct high cut at about 1.28 kHz (shown 13:32); OTT at 58 %; Fruity Balance; Simplon.
- [14:07](https://youtu.be/l_XjHmumqQk?t=847) Balance is his volume-automation tool for hard cuts and Simplon
  his usual filter; both end most of his chains (said).
- [14:30](https://youtu.be/l_XjHmumqQk?t=870) Plays chop and chords together and makes the two-sounds point.

## Tools the creator uses
- **DAW:** FL Studio 21, 128 BPM.
- **Instruments:** Output Arcade (vocal chop and the track's main vocal), Arturia Analog Lab V (chords). The
  channel rack also lists Serum, Nexus and Sylenth1 for parts not discussed (shown 2:30).
- **Effects:** DST multiband distortion (brightening), ValhallaDelay (tape quarter-note delay), Valhalla
  Supermassive (huge reverb-delay; free), FabFilter Pro-Q 3 (cuts and dynamic EQ), FocusOne (OTT-like shaper),
  Xfer OTT (bringing up tails), Fruity Balance (volume automation), FabFilter Simplon (filter automation).
