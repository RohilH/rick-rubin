## What it is about
Ryos starts from an empty FL Studio project at 128 BPM and builds a progressive house demo: break, build and
a 32-bar drop, in about 35 minutes of edited footage. He writes chords and a melody on a piano over a drone,
then builds the drop outward from the lead stack: leads, bass stack, drums, a "dance piano", and last the
build. He talks little and names few numbers, so most settings below were read off the screen. Nearly every
sound is a preset or a sample from his own packs; the lesson is the order of work, the roles of the layers and
the way every part is tied to the melody's rhythm. Pitches are given in Logic's naming (C3 = MIDI 60); FL shows
them two octaves higher.

## The main ideas
1. **Mood first, then notes.** A piano with a short reverb and compression plus a drone or atmosphere bed is
   the writing instrument; the key and mood were changed mid-idea when the chords turned emotional.
   [1:02](https://youtu.be/WnZ7GN1tpek?t=62), [2:26](https://youtu.be/WnZ7GN1tpek?t=146)
2. **One rhythm runs the whole drop.** The melody is two alternating one-bar rhythm cells; the bass, the dance
   piano stabs and the snare fill all copy that rhythm. [6:02](https://youtu.be/WnZ7GN1tpek?t=362),
   [16:16](https://youtu.be/WnZ7GN1tpek?t=976), [24:56](https://youtu.be/WnZ7GN1tpek?t=1496)
3. **Stacks are built by role, and redundant layers are deleted.** Leads: strings base, guitar, 80s lead,
   transient layer, plain synth layer, warm layer. Bass: sub, aggressive mid, thin "robot" mid, reese.
   [10:12](https://youtu.be/WnZ7GN1tpek?t=612), [15:55](https://youtu.be/WnZ7GN1tpek?t=955)
4. **Test the melody on the big sound early**, then build the drop before the build.
   [10:12](https://youtu.be/WnZ7GN1tpek?t=612), [29:59](https://youtu.be/WnZ7GN1tpek?t=1799)
5. **Do not squash the leads.** Group processing is an exciter, a light touch of a multi-effect, a gentle cut
   of harshness and a big delay. [13:58](https://youtu.be/WnZ7GN1tpek?t=838)
6. **Every layer has to leave room.** A hat is removed because the leads already carry enough white noise; a
   pad that takes too much space loses its lows and low mids. [23:48](https://youtu.be/WnZ7GN1tpek?t=1428),
   [28:14](https://youtu.be/WnZ7GN1tpek?t=1694)
7. **A build is mostly filtering.** Everything, including the break piano, is filtered away before the drop;
   then come a sweep, a snare roll that enters late, an impact, a riser and a pre-drop snare.
   [30:41](https://youtu.be/WnZ7GN1tpek?t=1841)
8. **A melody that survives a break is a good sign**, and a demo stops when the idea is all there; the rest is
   detail work for later. [20:55](https://youtu.be/WnZ7GN1tpek?t=1255), [34:13](https://youtu.be/WnZ7GN1tpek?t=2053)

## Step by step
- [1:02](https://youtu.be/WnZ7GN1tpek?t=62) Piano: Analog Lab V "German Grand Intimate" (shown). Insert chain:
  ValhallaVintageVerb, Plate, decay 0.55 s, predelay 10 ms, mix 52.8 %, then Waves RVox at about -13 (shown 1:38).
- [1:45](https://youtu.be/WnZ7GN1tpek?t=105) Drone bed under the piano: the sample "Prog Drone (Gmin)" from his
  packs, moved to B minor when the idea turned emotional (said). A second bed is tried and dropped.
- [3:09](https://youtu.be/WnZ7GN1tpek?t=189) Chords, one per bar: G, B sus4, D, A (IV, vi, I, V in D major), each
  voiced root, fifth, octave, third on top; chords 2 and 4 start an eighth early (shown 6:02).
- [3:50](https://youtu.be/WnZ7GN1tpek?t=230) Atmosphere: a brass swell sample labelled D#, pitched down to D
  (said); a reverse synth shot before the phrase, through Thermal, ValhallaDelay, Supermassive (shown 4:50).
- [4:58](https://youtu.be/WnZ7GN1tpek?t=298) Break bass: a Nexus patch he calls Swedish House Mafia style, one
  root per bar: G, B, D, A (said).
- [5:18](https://youtu.be/WnZ7GN1tpek?t=318) Melody on the piano, 8 bars, mostly chord tones; he tries a higher
  ending for bar 8 and postpones the decision (said). Rhythm read at 6:02 (shown).
- [7:19](https://youtu.be/WnZ7GN1tpek?t=439) Moving pad: Serum saw pad (2 voices, Low 24 filter) with the
  movement added after the synth: kHs Trance Gate, 16 steps on/off, mix 53 % (shown 8:10). Raised an octave.
- [8:25](https://youtu.be/WnZ7GN1tpek?t=505) "Analog drone" synth from an earlier track playing a small line;
  chain Decapitator, Supermassive, Pro-Q 3, PanMan, Kickstart 2 (shown 9:12). Then a small kick and claps
  where the build will start (9:07).
- [10:12](https://youtu.be/WnZ7GN1tpek?t=612) Lead stack, each layer playing the chords with the melody on top
  (shown 10:37): Nexus "Progressive Strings" (said), a Kontakt guitar with a low cut and a narrow dip at 3.1 kHz
  (shown 11:14), Serum "Full Nelson" 80s lead (shown 12:32), a Serum xylo patch an octave down for transient
  (shown 13:02), Spire for a plain synth layer, Nexus for warmth (shown 13:56). A vocal-like layer is rejected
  as redundant; one lead is later swapped for being "goofy".
- [13:58](https://youtu.be/WnZ7GN1tpek?t=838) Lead group: Noveltech Character, FocusOne ("just a taste"),
  Pro-Q 3 wide cut of about 1 dB in the upper mids, ValhallaDelay quarter-note stereo preset with the repeats
  band-limited to 330 Hz to 5.59 kHz (shown 14:54).
- [15:09](https://youtu.be/WnZ7GN1tpek?t=909) Drop started with the lead pattern, a dreamy chord synth and the
  drone. Bass from 15:55: roots in the melody's rhythm (shown 16:30), pitch-bend slides into changes (shown 16:52).
- [17:00](https://youtu.be/WnZ7GN1tpek?t=1020) Thin "robot" bass: Spire "BA Talking", ValhallaSpaceModulator
  (VariUp, mix 43 %, 0.27 Hz, depth 5 ms, feedback 90 %), then Pro-Q 3 low cut at 406 Hz, 48 dB/oct, then
  Thermal (shown 17:24 to 18:06). A high cut is added later (shown 19:34).
- [18:50](https://youtu.be/WnZ7GN1tpek?t=1130) Sub in Serum: a sine patch with attack; replaced by a cleaner
  sub with no attack (said). The aggressive bass is swapped for another (19:38).
- [20:00](https://youtu.be/WnZ7GN1tpek?t=1200) Reese from an older track, up an octave: Serum saw, 7 voices,
  MG Low 18, mono legato; chain Decapitator, CamelCrusher, two Pro-Q 3, Creative Filter, LFOTool (shown 20:26, 20:52).
- [21:10](https://youtu.be/WnZ7GN1tpek?t=1270) Drop drums in order: kick shortened (sample FXP_kick_club_01,
  shown), noise rides, one slice of a ride with a fade, claps, a shaker loop shifted down 478 Hz (shown 23:20),
  spray fill and clap fill; a closed hat removed for adding white noise over the leads (said).
- [24:08](https://youtu.be/WnZ7GN1tpek?t=1448) A perc top loop for sparkle with Kickstart 2 at 1/4, mix 100 %
  (shown 24:38). Last-bar fill: a snare following the melody's rhythm (said).
- [25:39](https://youtu.be/WnZ7GN1tpek?t=1539) Dance piano: Nexus "EDM Piano" layered with a tight supersaw as
  one unit, stabbing the chords in the melody's rhythm (shown 27:04); the melody is taken out of it.
- [28:14](https://youtu.be/WnZ7GN1tpek?t=1694) The chord pad takes too much room: lows and low mids out, high
  mids up (said; on a plug-in called DST, shown 28:36). Dance piano chain: Maserati GTi, Thermal for a
  bit-crush colour, Character, Pro-Q 3, ValhallaDelay "Instant Vibe", Kickstart 2 last (shown 29:54).
- [29:59](https://youtu.be/WnZ7GN1tpek?t=1799) Build, bars 17 to 24: Simplon filters and Endless Smile automated
  on the lead group, filters on build kick, clap, break bass and piano (shown 30:08, 31:33). Then the sweep, the
  snare roll with pitch automation, impact, riser, pre-drop fill (shown 31:48 to 33:10).
- [33:09](https://youtu.be/WnZ7GN1tpek?t=1989) Swaps the reverse synth, adds a hat loop where the melody enters,
  plays it back. Layout (shown 34:20, 35:35): bars 1 to 8 chords and drone, 9 to 16 melody with bass and hats,
  17 to 24 build, drop 25 to 57.

## Tools the creator uses
- **DAW:** FL Studio. **Synths:** Arturia Analog Lab V (piano), reFX Nexus 4 (break bass, lead strings, warm
  layer, EDM piano, supersaw), Serum (pad, two leads, mid bass, sub, reese), Reveal Sound Spire (lead layer,
  chord synth, talking bass), Kontakt 6 (guitar).
- **Effects:** ValhallaVintageVerb, ValhallaDelay, Valhalla Supermassive, ValhallaSpaceModulator, Waves RVox,
  Waves Maserati GTi, Noveltech Character, FocusOne, FabFilter Pro-Q 3 and Simplon, Output Thermal, Soundtoys
  Decapitator and PanMan, CamelCrusher, Sonalksis Creative Filter, Dada Life Endless Smile, ArtsAcoustic Reverb,
  Kilohearts Trance Gate and Frequency Shifter, Kickstart 2, LFOTool, a distortion called DST.
- **Samples:** his own Patreon and "Knight"/"LVL" packs (drone, atmos, claps, rides, fills, sweeps, snares,
  loops), a KSHMR shaker loop and snare, kicks named PH_KICK_02 (build) and FXP_kick_club_01 (drop).
