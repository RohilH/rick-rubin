## What it is about

His first tech house tutorial, two years before the "Full Tutorial": Ableton Live with Serum version 1 at
128 BPM. Four fast steps: drums with top layers, a square pluck bass and a rolling one-root bassline with
pitch bends, "the vibe" (a two-note techno riff on a compressed saw, and a big brass preset), and a vocal
from his pack. It adds what the 2025 series lacks: a second bassline type, a lead, a brass sound and a word
on section lengths. About a quarter of it sells his "Astral" pack and a contest; the last half minute is the
finished drop.

## The main ideas

1. **The bassline is the heartbeat**: it drives the whole song, so it gets the most care
   ([1:46](https://youtu.be/_4qJSUFao50?t=106)).
2. **Rhythm beats notes**: a riff from two notes, varied only by rhythm and octave, is enough
   ([4:03](https://youtu.be/_4qJSUFao50?t=243)).
3. **Low roots on purpose**: he likes D or D sharp as the root because it hits the subs hard
   ([2:52](https://youtu.be/_4qJSUFao50?t=172)).
4. **Pitch bend is a groove tool**: drawn over the bass clip, and with a wider range for slides on the lead
   ([3:13](https://youtu.be/_4qJSUFao50?t=193), [4:44](https://youtu.be/_4qJSUFao50?t=284)).
5. **Everything in eights**: the first section of a drop is eight bars; the riff alone, then a riser, then
   the drop, eight bars each ([1:15](https://youtu.be/_4qJSUFao50?t=75), [4:55](https://youtu.be/_4qJSUFao50?t=295)).
6. **Old-school techno sounds with a modern twist** are where he expects the genre to go
   ([3:42](https://youtu.be/_4qJSUFao50?t=222)).
7. **A catchy vocal runs through the whole track** ([6:05](https://youtu.be/_4qJSUFao50?t=365)).

## Step by step

1. Tempo: tech house is about 123 to 128 BPM; he uses 128 (said, [0:42](https://youtu.be/_4qJSUFao50?t=42)).
2. Kick on every beat, clap on 2 and 4, hat on the offbeat, rides in 8ths (said; captions shown at
   [1:10](https://youtu.be/_4qJSUFao50?t=70)). Duplicated to eight bars.
3. "Secret sauce": shakers, a percussion top loop for swing, panned rides; the shaker and the top loop cover
   only the second half of the eight bars (shown at [1:32](https://youtu.be/_4qJSUFao50?t=92)).
4. Bass sound: Serum init, Basic Shapes on the square, MG Low 12 with the cutoff at 205 Hz (shown at
   [2:05](https://youtu.be/_4qJSUFao50?t=125)); LFO 1 on the cutoff, ENV mode, 1/4, redrawn as a falling
   curve (shown at [2:20](https://youtu.be/_4qJSUFao50?t=140)); bend range 2 / −2 there.
5. Bassline: D1 and D#1; straight 8ths first, then a re-cut rhythm with octave pops to D2 (shown at
   [3:18](https://youtu.be/_4qJSUFao50?t=198) and [3:25](https://youtu.be/_4qJSUFao50?t=205)).
6. Pitch-bend ramp per bar in the clip envelope (shown at 3:18).
7. Riff inspired by "Satisfaction": D2, D1 and short stabs on F2, D#2, D2, in G minor (shown at
   [4:10](https://youtu.be/_4qJSUFao50?t=250)).
8. Its sound: an init saw with Serum's compressor in multiband mode, hall reverb and a 1/4 delay; mono; bend
   range 2 / −13 (shown at [4:35](https://youtu.be/_4qJSUFao50?t=275) and
   [4:50](https://youtu.be/_4qJSUFao50?t=290)).
9. Preset "BASS – Big Brass": two saws (one voice an octave up, eight voices), resonant MG Low 12, attack
   280 ms, mono legato, a 2-bar envelope LFO (shown at [5:30](https://youtu.be/_4qJSUFao50?t=330)).
10. A vocal sample from his pack over the drop ([6:05](https://youtu.be/_4qJSUFao50?t=365)); no processing shown.

## Tools the creator uses

- **Ableton Live**: arrangement, clip envelopes for pitch bend; return tracks named Reverb and Delay exist.
- **Serum (version 1)**: bass from init, the techno saw from init, his "Big Brass" preset.
- **His "Astral" pack** (500 licences, November 2023): drum samples, "Astral Top Loop 01 – 128BPM", presets,
  vocals. A producer contest with its free sample kit is announced.
