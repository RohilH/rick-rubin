## What it is about

The bass chapter of his tech house series, in Ableton Live 12 with Serum 2 at 130 BPM. It is the same footage
and narration as minutes 6 to 13 of "HOW TO TECH HOUSE | Full Tutorial" (see `how-to-tech-house.md`), cut out
as its own video: four bass "personalities", a pluck bass built from Serum's init patch, then an offbeat
bassline and its variations. Nothing in it is new against the full tutorial; this digest maps it to the cards
that already exist and records the few extra details the frames gave.

## The main ideas

1. **A bassline is two separate jobs**: the sound design and the MIDI pattern
   ([1:22](https://youtu.be/c_Y2MnnoNMQ?t=82)).
2. **The groove bass runs all the time; character basses sit on top of it**: an acid sound, a robotic
   stutter, a borrowed dubstep or bass house sound ([0:20](https://youtu.be/c_Y2MnnoNMQ?t=20) to
   [1:22](https://youtu.be/c_Y2MnnoNMQ?t=82)).
3. **Every sound is wavetable, filter, automation**: the wave is the character, the filter the depth, an LFO
   on the cutoff makes it alive ([1:43](https://youtu.be/c_Y2MnnoNMQ?t=103) to
   [3:07](https://youtu.be/c_Y2MnnoNMQ?t=187)).
4. **Envelope mode turns the LFO into a per-note pluck**, so the notes, not the LFO, set the rhythm
   ([3:48](https://youtu.be/c_Y2MnnoNMQ?t=228)).
5. **Start the pattern on the offbeats, then spice it**: double notes, move notes, octave jumps, one
   displaced note ([6:30](https://youtu.be/c_Y2MnnoNMQ?t=390) to [8:04](https://youtu.be/c_Y2MnnoNMQ?t=484)).

## Step by step

1. Four personalities, each with a title card: #1 The Groove, #2 Sci-Fi Acid, #3 Robo-Stutter (card shown at
   [1:06](https://youtu.be/c_Y2MnnoNMQ?t=66)), #4 BASS x House (card at
   [1:16](https://youtu.be/c_Y2MnnoNMQ?t=76)). #3 and #4 are heard under title cards only: no patch is shown.
2. Saw wave, low-pass filter, LFO 1 dragged to the cutoff, depth on the small ring
   ([1:43](https://youtu.be/c_Y2MnnoNMQ?t=103) to [3:07](https://youtu.be/c_Y2MnnoNMQ?t=187)).
3. LFO redrawn as a falling stab, rate changed, Envelope mode on
   ([3:27](https://youtu.be/c_Y2MnnoNMQ?t=207) to [4:08](https://youtu.be/c_Y2MnnoNMQ?t=248)).
4. Finishing: release up, mono, sustain down, decay tighter, sub oscillator, cutoff lower, random phase to 0
   and the phase moved by ear, filter drive up ([4:49](https://youtu.be/c_Y2MnnoNMQ?t=289) to
   [6:00](https://youtu.be/c_Y2MnnoNMQ?t=360)).
5. Pattern: D sharp on every offbeat for two bars, then doubles, moves, an octave jump and one displaced
   note ([6:30](https://youtu.be/c_Y2MnnoNMQ?t=390)). The finished clip (shown at
   [8:12](https://youtu.be/c_Y2MnnoNMQ?t=492)): D#0 and E0, one E1, one G#0, velocity 100, groove "Swing 16ths".
6. "Bass Pattern #2" ([8:18](https://youtu.be/c_Y2MnnoNMQ?t=498), shown): one pitch in straight 8ths with
   16th doubles, one octave-up note and one low note per phrase.
7. "Bass Pattern #3" ([8:23](https://youtu.be/c_Y2MnnoNMQ?t=503), shown; the clip is named "OMNIA Bass Groove
   (Night Rider 2.1) – 128BPM Amin"): each cycle opens with two 16ths on G0, jumps to C#1, then holds F#0
   for the rest. So a semitone-above pick-up into the root plus a pop on the fifth (inferred from the notes).
