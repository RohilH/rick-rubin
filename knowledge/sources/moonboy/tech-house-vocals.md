## What it is about

The vocal chapter of his tech house series: five ways to process a vocal (hard tuning, tuning from a MIDI
keyboard, formant shifting, vocoding, compression), a one-minute demo of the three core effects being
adjusted, and three ways to get a vocal. It is the last six minutes of "HOW TO TECH HOUSE | Full Tutorial"
(see `how-to-tech-house.md`) as its own video. The techniques are already in the knowledge base; this pass
read the one-minute demo frame by frame, which the first digest could not, and made one card from it.

## The main ideas

1. **The vocal is what people remember**, and with a driving beat and a catchy bass it is the third and
   last ingredient ([0:00](https://youtu.be/vDgVqHKPBIM?t=0)).
2. **Process it until it is yours**: chop, effect, flip, pitch; five named treatments
   ([1:02](https://youtu.be/vDgVqHKPBIM?t=62)).
3. **Playing the vocal's pitch from a keyboard** gives the dark, epic dance vocal
   ([1:23](https://youtu.be/vDgVqHKPBIM?t=83)).
4. **A vocoder makes the vocal a synth**, and chords into it make it melodic
   ([2:32](https://youtu.be/vDgVqHKPBIM?t=152), [3:08](https://youtu.be/vDgVqHKPBIM?t=188)).
5. **Compression is "the most essential"**: it fills the gaps and brings up the effects before it
   ([3:08](https://youtu.be/vDgVqHKPBIM?t=188)).
6. **A remix vocal is a fast way to start and a placeholder to replace**; hired vocalists and sample packs
   each have a cost ([4:28](https://youtu.be/vDgVqHKPBIM?t=268) to [6:11](https://youtu.be/vDgVqHKPBIM?t=371)).

## Step by step

1. Hard tune: Auto Shift, quantizer strength 100 %, smoothing 11 ms, root A, scale Major, formant −7.9 %,
   fully wet, on the acapella "Make That Paper Multiply (Wet) 130BPM Amin" (shown at
   [2:25](https://youtu.be/vDgVqHKPBIM?t=145)).
2. MIDI tune: arm an empty MIDI track, MIDI mode on in the tuner, choose that track as input (said,
   [1:23](https://youtu.be/vDgVqHKPBIM?t=83)). Shown at [1:46](https://youtu.be/vDgVqHKPBIM?t=106): attack and
   release 20 ms, mono, pitch-bend range 6 st, on a second acapella, "GROOVE Acapella (Close To You – Dry)
   128BPM Amaj", with the set at 128 BPM.
3. Formant shifting, deeper or higher; tools named: Ableton Shifter, AlterBoy, FL Studio's Edison (said,
   [1:53](https://youtu.be/vDgVqHKPBIM?t=113)).
4. Vocoder: the carrier menu goes from Noise to Pitch Tracking (shown at
   [2:40](https://youtu.be/vDgVqHKPBIM?t=160) and [2:58](https://youtu.be/vDgVqHKPBIM?t=178)): tracking 80 to
   500 Hz, saw, pitch −12, Enhance, 20 bands, release 22.8 ms, mix 80.5 %.
5. Compression: OTT after the vocoder ([3:08](https://youtu.be/vDgVqHKPBIM?t=188)).
6. One-minute demo of tune + vocoder + OTT ([3:29](https://youtu.be/vDgVqHKPBIM?t=209) to
   [4:28](https://youtu.be/vDgVqHKPBIM?t=268)): five readable settings, see the new card. OTT here is
   Ableton's Multiband Dynamics preset: amount 96 to 100 %, time 129 to 134 %, crossovers 88 Hz and 2.5 kHz,
   output +9.5 dB (shown at [3:35](https://youtu.be/vDgVqHKPBIM?t=215)).
7. Where vocals come from: vocalist, remix, samples ([4:28](https://youtu.be/vDgVqHKPBIM?t=268)).

## Tools the creator uses

- **Ableton Live 12 (beta)**: Auto Shift (tuning, MIDI tuning, formant), Vocoder, Multiband Dynamics "OTT".
- **Named for formant**: Ableton Shifter, Soundtoys Little AlterBoy, FL Studio Edison.
- **His packs**: KULTURE and GROOVE acapellas, Project X (vocals; 400 licences).
