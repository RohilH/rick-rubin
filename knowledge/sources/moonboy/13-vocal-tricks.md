## What it is about

A compilation of his vocal sound-design tricks, cut together from several earlier videos (the acapellas and
sets on screen are at 130, 135 and about 158 BPM; the bus section is from his 126 BPM melodic house project).
Almost all of them are one idea in different clothes: take a sustained vocal, add space, then shape its
volume in rhythm. The parts: a reverb-and-tremolo vocal chop, a pad made from a vocal, rhythmic volume
presets, the "infinite vocal choir" in a sampler, the stutter house vocal, and the shared "butterfly" bus.
The tricks are not numbered on screen, so the thirteen cannot be listed one to one. About a minute sells his
"Euphoria" pack.

## The main ideas

1. **Space first, then rhythm**: reverb (and delay) make a vocal a sustained sound; a volume shape then
   gives it a pulse ([0:40](https://youtu.be/7wcJShg8tn4?t=40)).
2. **A vocal can be the pad**: reverb fully wet, recorded to audio, is a pad in the right key
   ([2:45](https://youtu.be/7wcJShg8tn4?t=165)).
3. **Volume shaping is a rhythm instrument**: an auto-panner at zero phase, or an LFO on level, turns any
   held sound into a part; off-grid shapes carry swing ([4:31](https://youtu.be/7wcJShg8tn4?t=271),
   [9:58](https://youtu.be/7wcJShg8tn4?t=598)).
4. **Resample and play it**: once the effect is audio, a sampler loop makes it an instrument with harmony
   ([6:43](https://youtu.be/7wcJShg8tn4?t=403), [8:52](https://youtu.be/7wcJShg8tn4?t=532)).
5. **Long phrases shape best**, and two stacked shapes on one knob sound more organic than one
   ([9:50](https://youtu.be/7wcJShg8tn4?t=590), [12:01](https://youtu.be/7wcJShg8tn4?t=721)).
6. **Write around an acapella, then remove it**, and the instrumental left is an original
   ([2:10](https://youtu.be/7wcJShg8tn4?t=130)). He also insists the viewer try at least one trick.

## Step by step

1. Vocal chop: clean melody, lots of reverb (ValhallaRoom, mix 97 %, decay 5.57 s: shown at
   [0:50](https://youtu.be/7wcJShg8tn4?t=50)), tremolo. Full chain at
   [1:15](https://youtu.be/7wcJShg8tn4?t=75): Auto-Tune Artist, ValhallaRoom, Auto Pan (amount 23 %, 1/4),
   OTT (amount 31 %).
2. Pad from a vocal: any reverb, decay and wet at maximum, freeze or export
   ([2:45](https://youtu.be/7wcJShg8tn4?t=165); shown at [3:15](https://youtu.be/7wcJShg8tn4?t=195)).
3. "Hover": his SerumFX tremolo preset on that pad (shown at [4:25](https://youtu.be/7wcJShg8tn4?t=265)).
4. Rhythm presets: a volume LFO with off-grid, swung peaks; the idea borrowed from Fred again.. morphing a
   percussion loop with a vocal ([4:31](https://youtu.be/7wcJShg8tn4?t=271); "Swing Cut 1" shown at
   [5:20](https://youtu.be/7wcJShg8tn4?t=320)).
5. Infinite choir: reverb (decay 12.9 s, wet 52 %: shown at [6:30](https://youtu.be/7wcJShg8tn4?t=390)) and
   delay; resample; sampler with a back-and-forth sustain loop and crossfade; Auto Pan as gate (shown at
   [8:40](https://youtu.be/7wcJShg8tn4?t=520), [9:00](https://youtu.be/7wcJShg8tn4?t=540)); a second copy
   for harmony ([6:02](https://youtu.be/7wcJShg8tn4?t=362) to [9:17](https://youtu.be/7wcJShg8tn4?t=557)).
6. Stutter house: Auto Pan, amount 86.5 %, phase 0°, synced; a little phase for width (shown at
   [10:25](https://youtu.be/7wcJShg8tn4?t=625)).
7. Advanced stutter in SerumFX: LFO 1 at 1/16 on the input level (shown at
   [11:40](https://youtu.be/7wcJShg8tn4?t=700)), LFO 2 at 1/4 on the envelope sustain as a duck (shown at
   [12:40](https://youtu.be/7wcJShg8tn4?t=760)), a macro on both.
8. The butterfly bus, repeated from the melodic house video ([13:32](https://youtu.be/7wcJShg8tn4?t=812)).

## Tools the creator uses

- **Ableton Live**: Auto Pan (tremolo, gate, duck), Reverb, Sampler (looping), resampling, OTT preset.
- **ValhallaRoom** (the big vocal reverb), **Antares Auto-Tune Artist** (tuning), **SerumFX** (LFO volume
  shaping; his presets "SFX – HOVER" and "FX – Swing Cut 1"). Mentioned, not shown: a "morph" plug-in that
  Fred again.. uses to combine two sounds.
- **His packs**: Euphoria (free beta: vocals, presets, project files), a SerumFX preset pack; acapellas "No
  One Like U" (130 BPM, F major) and "With You" (135 BPM).
