## What it is about

A full track from first beat to two drops, in Ableton Live with Serum version 1 at 126 BPM in G minor. The
idea is a blend: a melodic, trance-coloured first drop and a bass house second drop, both grown from one
chord progression. Five steps: drums, the melody (chords, a pluck, a mega saw, a bass layer), a shared
effect bus he calls the butterfly effect, a vocal hook, then the two drops. The last four and a half
minutes are the finished track playing; about two minutes sell his "Matrix" pack and a contest. This is the
closest of his videos to progressive house.

## The main ideas

1. **Start with a chord progression; the whole song is made from it**: bottom notes for bass and pads, top
   notes for leads, the chord for everything wide ([1:45](https://youtu.be/woNtFzXYB5Y?t=105),
   [2:20](https://youtu.be/woNtFzXYB5Y?t=140)).
2. **Build up in layers of register**: pluck for eight bars, then wide chords with a texture layer inside
   the synth, then a bass layer under them, each clearing room for the next
   ([5:11](https://youtu.be/woNtFzXYB5Y?t=311), [6:13](https://youtu.be/woNtFzXYB5Y?t=373)).
3. **One bus, one movement**: route synths and vocal to the same bus and move its volume, pan and filter, so
   they sound like one thing ([7:58](https://youtu.be/woNtFzXYB5Y?t=478),
   [11:33](https://youtu.be/woNtFzXYB5Y?t=693)).
4. **Two drops, two characters, same harmony**: the second drop is heavier but takes its notes from the
   same chords ([13:55](https://youtu.be/woNtFzXYB5Y?t=835), [17:01](https://youtu.be/woNtFzXYB5Y?t=1021)).
5. **Crush layers together**: distortion on the group, not on each synth, makes two sounds one
   ([14:56](https://youtu.be/woNtFzXYB5Y?t=896)).

## Step by step

1. Tempo 126 (house is 120 to 130, said). Kick on every beat, a snap on 2 and 4, a hat loop on the offbeats,
   rides (said and shown at [1:15](https://youtu.be/woNtFzXYB5Y?t=75)). The snap is nudged off the kick "for
   more crunch" (said).
2. A heavier beat for drop 2: heavier samples, groove in the hats, a crash, more rides, a fill at the end
   (said, [1:24](https://youtu.be/woNtFzXYB5Y?t=84)).
3. Chords on a piano: Gm, Dm, E flat, F, B flat, Dm, E flat over eight bars, each as root, fifth and tenth
   (shown at [2:15](https://youtu.be/woNtFzXYB5Y?t=135) and [5:50](https://youtu.be/woNtFzXYB5Y?t=350)).
4. Trance pluck from root and top note in a 3+3+2 rhythm (shown at
   [3:58](https://youtu.be/woNtFzXYB5Y?t=238)); cutoff automated upward; a four-register variation (shown at
   [5:03](https://youtu.be/woNtFzXYB5Y?t=303)).
5. Mega saw: two unison oscillators, MIDI doubled an octave down, attack, release, filter, a noise-slot
   texture layer; then the chords are high-passed and a bass layer plays the roots one or two octaves down
   ([5:11](https://youtu.be/woNtFzXYB5Y?t=311) to [7:38](https://youtu.be/woNtFzXYB5Y?t=458)).
6. Bus with SerumFX: LFO 1 on volume at 5.1 Hz unsynced (shown at
   [9:30](https://youtu.be/woNtFzXYB5Y?t=570)), rate automated, LFO 2 on pan, LFO 3 on a low-pass around
   11 kHz (shown at [10:40](https://youtu.be/woNtFzXYB5Y?t=640)).
7. Vocal hook written by a collaborator, routed into the same bus when the chords enter
   ([11:08](https://youtu.be/woNtFzXYB5Y?t=668)).
8. Drop 1: synth, vocal, drums and bass together ([13:35](https://youtu.be/woNtFzXYB5Y?t=815)).
9. Drop 2 bass: sine sub and saw lead in a group through Tube distortion, hyper/dimension, multiband
   compressor (gain 9.9 dB shown at [15:45](https://youtu.be/woNtFzXYB5Y?t=945)), delay, reverb; level LFOs;
   roots in the sub, chord tops in the lead ([14:15](https://youtu.be/woNtFzXYB5Y?t=855) to
   [17:22](https://youtu.be/woNtFzXYB5Y?t=1042)). The finished track plays from
   [18:03](https://youtu.be/woNtFzXYB5Y?t=1083).

## Tools the creator uses

- **Ableton Live**: grouping, routing to a bus/return, automation lanes; two return tracks read
  "ValhallaRoom" (small text at [17:15](https://youtu.be/woNtFzXYB5Y?t=1035)).
- **Serum (v1)** for every synth and bass; **SerumFX** as the bus effect and the group distortion chain.
- **His packs**: Matrix (free beta: pluck preset, 150 synth and FX presets, vocals), Eternity (noise and
  texture layers). A producer contest is announced.
