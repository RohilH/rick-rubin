## What it is about
A fast tour of a festival-style bass house track in Ableton Live with Serum (version 1), at 126 BPM in
F sharp minor. Five steps: drums, "the vibe" (a trance-style melody, a pad and a rave lead), the bass (a
mid-range "space laser" and a separate sub), a vocal with two effect racks, and a drop made of chopped
one-shots with A and B sections. Most settings are shown rather than said, and much of the sound comes from
presets in his "Astral" pack. No mixdown or master is shown; the last two minutes are the finished track.

## The main ideas
1. **Bass house is a house beat carrying sounds from other genres.** He names festival bass house (dubstep
   basses), UK house, ethereal bass house (trance melodies and vocals), speed house and complextro, and
   mixes them ([0:21](https://youtu.be/3-0JCDyiPaw?t=21)).
2. **Emotion comes from trance, weight from the bass.** The breakdown is a trance melody with a pad; the
   drop keeps the same notes but in heavy sounds ([2:14](https://youtu.be/3-0JCDyiPaw?t=134),
   [5:05](https://youtu.be/3-0JCDyiPaw?t=305)).
3. **Detuned saw voices are the base of pad, lead and bass alike**: more voices plus detune gives movement
   and width ([3:58](https://youtu.be/3-0JCDyiPaw?t=238), [5:05](https://youtu.be/3-0JCDyiPaw?t=305)).
4. **Find the one characteristic move of a sound, then stack ordinary effects on it**: for the laser bass
   that is a filter swept by a per-note envelope; compression, chorus, reverb and delay make it big
   ([5:27](https://youtu.be/3-0JCDyiPaw?t=327)).
5. **The sub is its own instrument**: triangle wave, distortion, and its own rhythm from volume ducking and
   pitch bends on the offbeat ([6:13](https://youtu.be/3-0JCDyiPaw?t=373)).
6. **Sub and lead do not have to play the same note**: put the sub a third under the lead and a heavy drop
   gains harmony ([10:50](https://youtu.be/3-0JCDyiPaw?t=650)).
7. **A drop is two answers, A and B**, alternating every two bars, and the B half can be a collage of
   one-shots where each slot of the grid is a different sound ([10:07](https://youtu.be/3-0JCDyiPaw?t=607),
   [11:35](https://youtu.be/3-0JCDyiPaw?t=695)).
8. **Level is part of the drum sound**: cymbals and rides sit far under kick and clap
   ([1:58](https://youtu.be/3-0JCDyiPaw?t=118)).

## Step by step
1. **Tempo and beat** ([1:05](https://youtu.be/3-0JCDyiPaw?t=65)). Bass house is 126 to 128 BPM, he uses 126
   (said). Kick every beat, clap on 2 and 4, hats on the offbeats (said and captioned).
2. **Drum extras** ([1:38](https://youtu.be/3-0JCDyiPaw?t=98)). Crash on beat 1; rides (he says a 1/8
   pattern; the clips sit one per beat) through an Auto Pan: amount 43.7 %, rate 1/2, phase 180°, shape
   15.9 %, offset 83°, inverted (shown at [1:47](https://youtu.be/3-0JCDyiPaw?t=107)). The clap starts a few
   milliseconds before the kick (caption at [1:49](https://youtu.be/3-0JCDyiPaw?t=109)). Accent snares on the
   16ths either side of beat 3 in bar 1, one in bar 3, a snare pair in the last beat of bar 4, and a reversed
   kick filling that last beat (shown at [1:52](https://youtu.be/3-0JCDyiPaw?t=112)).
3. **Levels** ([1:58](https://youtu.be/3-0JCDyiPaw?t=118)). On-screen guide: kick 0 dB, clap 0 dB, crashes
   −12 dB, rides −16 dB.
4. **Other beats** ([2:05](https://youtu.be/3-0JCDyiPaw?t=125)). Speed house at 150 BPM; a garage beat at 120
   to 130 BPM from a full drum loop over an 808 (captions only).
5. **Cosmic lead** ([2:39](https://youtu.be/3-0JCDyiPaw?t=159)). Preset "PLUCK - Cosmic": oscillator A with 7
   voices, oscillator B a saw an octave up with 4 voices, sub an octave down, MG Low 6 filter, LFO 1 as a
   falling one-shot envelope at 1/4 on the cutoff; hyper/dimension, chorus, delay (1/4), hall reverb, phaser
   (shown at [2:47](https://youtu.be/3-0JCDyiPaw?t=167) and [2:52](https://youtu.be/3-0JCDyiPaw?t=172)).
6. **Trance melody** ([2:55](https://youtu.be/3-0JCDyiPaw?t=175)). Root F#2 every three 16ths, restarting
   each bar; a second note on the 16th after each root (E3); eight bars; scale set to F# minor; then the top
   notes are moved into a melody and the bottom notes into a rising line F#, G#, A, C# (shown at
   [3:06](https://youtu.be/3-0JCDyiPaw?t=186) and [3:33](https://youtu.be/3-0JCDyiPaw?t=213)).
7. **Cosmic pad** ([3:36](https://youtu.be/3-0JCDyiPaw?t=216)). Long bass notes D1, E1, F#1 under the melody
   (shown at [3:56](https://youtu.be/3-0JCDyiPaw?t=236)). Sound: one saw an octave down, unison 15, detune
   swept between 0.19 and 0.47, MG Low 12, phaser (158 Hz) and hall reverb (shown at
   [4:08](https://youtu.be/3-0JCDyiPaw?t=248), [4:22](https://youtu.be/3-0JCDyiPaw?t=262)).
8. **Rave lead** ([4:30](https://youtu.be/3-0JCDyiPaw?t=270)). Preset "LEAD - Rave" plays the melody's notes
   long in the build: two oscillators with 2 and 16 voices, a phaser-type filter, LFO 1 as an envelope at
   1/16 (shown at [4:41](https://youtu.be/3-0JCDyiPaw?t=281)).
9. **Space laser bass** ([4:55](https://youtu.be/3-0JCDyiPaw?t=295)). Pattern: two 16ths and an 8th on every
   beat, E2 then F#2 then G#2 (shown at [5:10](https://youtu.be/3-0JCDyiPaw?t=310)). Two saws, 2 and 16
   voices (shown at [5:26](https://youtu.be/3-0JCDyiPaw?t=326)). Serum's EQ with a low cut whose frequency
   LFO 1 sweeps; the LFO is a steep falling curve in envelope mode at 1/4 (shown at
   [5:35](https://youtu.be/3-0JCDyiPaw?t=335), [5:40](https://youtu.be/3-0JCDyiPaw?t=340)). Finished chain:
   EQ, filter, phaser, chorus, hyper (4 voices) and dimension, multiband compressor, plate reverb, 1/8 delay,
   soft-clip distortion (shown at [6:03](https://youtu.be/3-0JCDyiPaw?t=363)).
10. **Sub** ([6:13](https://youtu.be/3-0JCDyiPaw?t=373)). Triangle wave, tube distortion, LFO 1 as a steep
    one-shot on master tune (amount about 22) for a pitch drop (shown at
    [6:54](https://youtu.be/3-0JCDyiPaw?t=414)); in the track: mono, legato, held notes, an LFO shape ducking
    the level every beat and a pitch-bend spike on every offbeat (shown at
    [7:01](https://youtu.be/3-0JCDyiPaw?t=421)).
11. **Vocal** ([7:19](https://youtu.be/3-0JCDyiPaw?t=439)). SerumFX rack "SFX - VOCAL RACK": hyper/dimension,
    low-cut EQ, 1/4 ping-pong delay, hall reverb, compressor (shown at
    [7:43](https://youtu.be/3-0JCDyiPaw?t=463)). A second rack, "SFX - Add Ethereal" (reverb-type filter,
    unsynced delay, flanger, hall reverb), turns a copy into a pad and runs in parallel under the clean
    vocal (shown at [8:10](https://youtu.be/3-0JCDyiPaw?t=490), [8:20](https://youtu.be/3-0JCDyiPaw?t=500)).
12. **Drop B: chops** ([10:07](https://youtu.be/3-0JCDyiPaw?t=607)). Five lanes of one-shots (bass, vocal
    chop, synth, arp, synth), one sound per slot; tips on screen: chops in key, compress them together,
    sidechain (shown at [10:43](https://youtu.be/3-0JCDyiPaw?t=643)).
13. **Lead against sub** ([10:50](https://youtu.be/3-0JCDyiPaw?t=650)). Lead E2, F#2, G#2 over sub C#, D, E
    in the same rhythm (shown at [11:03](https://youtu.be/3-0JCDyiPaw?t=663)).
14. **Structure** ([11:35](https://youtu.be/3-0JCDyiPaw?t=695)). Markers Drop A, Drop B, A, B, two bars each,
    from bar 89 (shown at [11:53](https://youtu.be/3-0JCDyiPaw?t=713)). The synth, bass, drop and FX groups
    are routed into one track named "Sidechain"; drums and vocals go straight to the master (shown).

## Tools the creator uses
- **Ableton Live**: arrangement, Auto Pan (rides), Audio Effect Rack (parallel vocal chains), MIDI pitch-bend
  envelopes, scale highlighting in the piano roll.
- **Serum (v1)**: every synth and bass; **SerumFX**: the two vocal racks.
- **His pack "Astral"** (500 licences): drums (Astral Crash 01, Astral Clap 01), presets "PLUCK - Cosmic",
  "LEAD - Rave", "SFX - VOCAL RACK", "SFX - Add Ethereal", vocal samples, 808 MIDI; a garage drum loop.

## Not covered or unclear
- No mix or master chain, no build-up or riser design, no intro or outro; the "Sidechain" bus is visible but
  what is on it is not.
- "Triplet" in his words is not triplets on screen: the melody is cells of three 16ths, the bass two 16ths
  and an 8th per beat. Rides: he says 1/8, the clips are one per beat; which is heard is not settled.
- Knob values without a tooltip (filter cutoffs, effect mixes, LFO depths) are not readable; the clap's
  lead over the kick has no number. The grid length of the chop section is not readable.
- Speed house and garage beats appear for a few seconds each with no explanation.
