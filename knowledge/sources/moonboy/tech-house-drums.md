## What it is about

The drum chapter of his tech house series: what tech house is, the 130 BPM house beat in four lanes, a few
ghost hits, then three "bonus" mix tricks on the drum group. It is the opening six minutes of "HOW TO TECH
HOUSE | Full Tutorial" (see `how-to-tech-house.md`) released first as its own video, with a one-minute
announcement of his Project X masterclass in the middle. No technique in it is new against the full tutorial.

## The main ideas

1. **Tech house is house made minimal and groovy**: a simple drum beat, a deep bassline, a catchy vocal,
   and the question is how much flavour those few parts can carry
   ([0:20](https://youtu.be/JOLlIBJNRts?t=20), [0:40](https://youtu.be/JOLlIBJNRts?t=40)).
2. **Styles he names**: a European style, a funky one, a hard one, and a trap and house blend that starts as
   a remix of a hip-hop song and drops into trap or dubstep ([1:04](https://youtu.be/JOLlIBJNRts?t=64)). That
   last one is the shape of Rohil's current remix.
3. **Sample quality comes first**: build the beat from good one-shots, one track per sound
   ([1:44](https://youtu.be/JOLlIBJNRts?t=104)).
4. **Treat the kit as one instrument**: group it so effects and automation act on all drums at once
   ([5:15](https://youtu.be/JOLlIBJNRts?t=315)).
5. **Loud and clean comes from clipping the group, not compressing it**
   ([6:02](https://youtu.be/JOLlIBJNRts?t=362)).

## Step by step

1. Tempo: house is 120 to 130 BPM; he sets 130 (said, [1:24](https://youtu.be/JOLlIBJNRts?t=84)).
2. Tracks named Kick, Clap, Hat, Cymbal, Snare, colour-coded (said and shown).
3. Kick on every beat, clap on every second beat, hat on every offbeat, crash on beat 1; two bars looped,
   then duplicated to four ([2:45](https://youtu.be/JOLlIBJNRts?t=165)).
4. Percussive extras: a snare and another clap on weak 16ths, more at the end of the phrase
   ([3:27](https://youtu.be/JOLlIBJNRts?t=207); lanes shown at [3:48](https://youtu.be/JOLlIBJNRts?t=228)).
5. Group as "Drums"; snare down to about −6 dB, cymbal down (said,
   [5:35](https://youtu.be/JOLlIBJNRts?t=335)).
6. Glue Compressor on the group with Soft Clip on (said, [6:02](https://youtu.be/JOLlIBJNRts?t=362));
   threshold 0 dB, make-up 0 dB, range 70 dB, dry/wet 100 % (shown at
   [7:42](https://youtu.be/JOLlIBJNRts?t=462)).
7. Thin clap: limiter, ceiling −3 dB, input gain up, release down (said,
   [6:24](https://youtu.be/JOLlIBJNRts?t=384)).
8. Auto Filter on the group, band-pass, 1.21 kHz, resonance 31 % (shown at 7:42); its on/off is automated
   (said, [7:05](https://youtu.be/JOLlIBJNRts?t=425)).

## Tools the creator uses

- **Ableton Live 12**: Glue Compressor (soft clip), Limiter (clap), Auto Filter (band-pass fills).
- **His "FREE House Drum Kit (Project X Beta)"**, contents shown at 3:48: kicks (Classic, Deep, Stomp), snares
  (Hip Hop, Resonant, Rim, Perc), claps (Snappy, Thick), a house hat, a house crash, a noise hit, and loops at
  126 and 130 BPM (drum, top, percussion, clap and hat loops, a noise ambience).
- **Project X** (400 licences) and its masterclass: announced, not used.
