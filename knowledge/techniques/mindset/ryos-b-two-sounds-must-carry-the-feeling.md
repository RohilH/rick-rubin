## What it is
A test for the start of a section: the main one or two sounds, alone, must already state the theme and the
mood. Layers support a feeling that exists; they do not create one.

## When to use it
At the start of every break, drop or new idea, and whenever a project has many tracks and still feels empty.

## How
1. His break's identity is two sounds with no layers: a vocal chop and a chord sound (said,
   [14:30](https://youtu.be/l_XjHmumqQk?t=870)).
2. The habit he warns against, from his own past and from younger producers: adding sound after sound hoping
   one of them brings the "magic" (said, [14:52](https://youtu.be/l_XjHmumqQk?t=892)).
3. Why it fails: many sounds and many plug-ins make clutter, and the feeling does not appear that way (said,
   [15:13](https://youtu.be/l_XjHmumqQk?t=913)).
4. Instead spend the time finding one or two high-quality sounds that have the feeling, before trying other
   sounds and layers (said, [15:13](https://youtu.be/l_XjHmumqQk?t=913)).
5. Do not build the track out if the main ingredients do not have it yet; that time is wasted (said,
   [15:34](https://youtu.be/l_XjHmumqQk?t=934)).
6. Only then the supporting cast: in his break a backing synth, an accent synth, an arp, a tension string, a
   reese and a dirty bass (track list shown, [1:40](https://youtu.be/l_XjHmumqQk?t=100)).

## In Rohil's setup
This matches his preferences file (a few strong, simple parts over many clever ones). Make it a gate:
- Before layering, bounce only the two core tracks of the section: `rick-rubin logic bounce core.wav --bars
  A-B --tracks "Vocal Chop" Chords`, and give it to Rohil. If he does not like the two alone, change the
  sounds; do not add a third.
- When choosing those sounds, shortlist and compare (`rick-rubin serum search`, `rick-rubin serum audition`,
  `rick-rubin audition` for samples); the search is the work at this stage.
- `rick-rubin logic inspect` shows how many tracks play in the section. A long list with no clear core two
  is the warning sign.

## How to check it
This is an ear test, so the check is his verdict on the two-track bounce (`rick-rubin verdict`). A partial
number: on `rick-rubin spectrum` the two core sounds should already cover the mid range between them, so later
layers are filling gaps and adding width, not supplying the body.

## Why it works
A listener remembers one or two things per section. If those are strong, support makes them bigger; if they
are weak, more layers only hide the problem and use up space in the mix.
