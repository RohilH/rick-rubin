## What it is
A cousin of the feel-good loop with two minor chords in it and a minor v at the end: joy with "a touch of sadness
and sorrow" in his words.

## When to use it
Emotional progressive house: a breakdown that should feel wistful rather than triumphant.

## How
1. Natural minor; degrees 6, 1, 7, 5 (said; in G# minor: E, G#m, F#, D#m). The overlay at
   [6:38](https://youtu.be/_yw85rAb6cw?t=398) labels the last chord D# major, but the notes drawn measure as D#
   minor, the diatonic v (read from note spacing, [7:00](https://youtu.be/_yw85rAb6cw?t=420)).
2. Chord lengths over four bars: 5 beats, 2 beats, 7 beats, 2 beats. The tonic and the v are short passing chords;
   VI and VII carry the time (shown [7:00](https://youtu.be/_yw85rAb6cw?t=420) on Ableton's ruler).
3. Third of each chord up an octave: he likes the third as the top note (said,
   [6:51](https://youtu.be/_yw85rAb6cw?t=411)).
4. Pedals on scale degree 3 and the fifth above it, degree 7 (said; shown
   [7:00](https://youtu.be/_yw85rAb6cw?t=420): B through the loop, F# above).
5. Colours (inferred): VI add9, i as m7, VII with an added fourth, v with a flat sixth, which is the relative
   major's maj7 in first inversion (D#m + B = Bmaj7/D#).
6. A sad-sounding synth patch (said; no settings shown).
7. To vary it (ours): try the major V he labelled for the last chord on the final pass only; it pulls much harder
   back to the tonic and needs the degree 7 pedal dropped for that bar (it clashes with the raised leading note).

## In Rohil's setup
The short chords are easy to lose in a busy mix: keep the pad's release short enough that the two-beat chords are
heard as changes. Roots above C2 when the bass is in.

## How to check it
`rick-rubin score <dir> --tracks <chords>`: changes at bar 2 beat 2, bar 2 beat 4 and bar 4 beat 3. Bounce it
against the feel-good card's version in the same key and sound; Rohil should hear this one as the sadder.

## Why it works
Half the chords are minor and the loop ends on v, which does not pull home the way a major V does, so it hangs. The
long VI and VII still give lift (inferred).
