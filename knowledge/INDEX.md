# Index of the knowledge base

What is here: 190 technique cards and 27 source digests from three creators (Ryos: progressive house and big
room; Moonboy: tech house, bass house, melodic house, vocals; Alex Rome: theory, mixing, sound design, song
systems). Section 1 lists every card once, by topic, with what it gives you. Section 2 lists the same cards by
genre tag. Section 3 lists the digests. Section 4 lists pairs of cards that teach the same thing.

Read with: [PRINCIPLES.md](PRINCIPLES.md) (themes across creators), [FINISHED.md](FINISHED.md) (is this track
finished?), [TOOLS.md](TOOLS.md) (what they use and what Rohil has), and the genre guides
[tech house](genres/tech-house.md), [bass house](genres/bass-house.md), [progressive house](genres/progressive-house.md).
Every card is `status: untested`: nothing has been run in Logic and judged by ear yet.
